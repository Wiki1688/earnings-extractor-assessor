ALIGN TECHNOLOGY ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

Clear Aligner Revenue Growth, Improved Profitability and Continued Capital Return Demonstrate Progress on Strategic Priorities


•Q3'26 total revenues were $1.015 billion, up 1.9% year-over-year
•Q3'26 Clear Aligner revenues of $839.3 million increased 4.2% year-over-year, and Clear Aligner volume increased 6.6% year-over-year to 690.6 thousand cases
•Q3'26 Imaging Systems and CAD/CAM Services revenues were $175.5 million, down 7.6% year-over-year
•Q3'26 gross margin was 68.7%. On a non-GAAP basis, Q3'26 gross margin was 70.4%(1), flat year-over-year or up approximately 0.7 points at constant currency
•Q3'26 operating margin was 16.7%, compared with 9.7% in Q3'25. Non-GAAP operating margin was 24.4%(1), up 0.5 points year-over-year or up approximately 1.4 points at constant currency
•Q3'26 diluted net income per share was $1.90, compared with $0.78 in Q3'25. Non-GAAP diluted net income per share was $2.80(1), compared with $2.61 in Q3'25

TEMPE, Ariz., October 28, 2026 -- Align Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: ALGN), a leading global medical device company that designs, manufactures, and sells the Invisalign® System of clear aligners, iTero™ intraoral scanners, and exocad™ CAD/CAM software for digital orthodontics and restorative dentistry, today reported financial results for the third quarter ("Q3'26").

Commenting on Align's Q3'26 results, Align Technology President and CEO Joe Hogan said, “We delivered solid third-quarter results, with Q3 revenues and Clear Aligner volumes in line with our outlook and non-GAAP operating margin was above our prior guidance. Clear Aligner volumes reflect record doctor submitters, record third-quarter Clear Aligner shipments, improved utilization, and broad-based international growth. For the first time in 6 quarters, orthodontist shipments in North America increased year-over-year, and DSO shipments were up double-digits, across both orthodontist and GP customers. Our focus remains on expanding iTero’s reach and in Q3, the number of scanner units placed—including leased, rented and certified pre-owned systems increased double-digits year-over-year, to more than 157,000 systems globally. From a profitability perspective, non-GAAP gross margin was 70.4%, modestly below our expectation, reflecting higher fuel-related transportation costs, lower Clear Aligner ASPs and geographic and product mix. Disciplined expense management and productivity improvements contributed to non-GAAP operating margin of 24.4%, above our outlook. Overall, Q3 demonstrated continued Clear Aligner growth, expansion of our digital platform and customer footprint, and improved profitability."

Continued Hogan, "We also have a significant innovation pipeline ahead of us. We continue to be excited about our next-gen direct fabrication 3D printing materials platform, called NXT Technology. NXT Technology unlocks greater control, consistency, and precision across appliance design-enabling an entirely new level of Invisalign® performance and personalization. We are broadening the application of 3D printing across the Invisalign portfolio, as well as expanding into new categories: Invisalign® Specifix attachment system expands our direct 3D-printing capabilities beyond aligners and appliances into the attachment system itself. This week, we will begin a U.S. pilot of a new product Invisalign® Sports Guard -- a custom direct 3D printed sports mouth guards -- made from an iTero™ scan. Sports mouth guards represent a large and growing market opportunity amid increasing awareness of concussion risks in contact sports. There is also significant overlap with our kids and teens demographic, and we expect it to drive awareness and utilization of Invisalign treatment through Invisalign doctors.”

Q3'26 total revenues were $1,014.8 million, down 3.9% sequentially and up 1.9% year-over-year. Q3'26 total revenues were unfavorably impacted by foreign exchange by approximately $4.7 million, or 0.5% sequentially, and unfavorably impacted by approximately $5.0 million, or 0.5% year-over-year.(1) Q3'26 Clear Aligner revenues were $839.3 million, down 3.6% sequentially and up 4.2% year-over-year. Q3'26 Clear Aligner revenues were unfavorably impacted by foreign exchange by approximately $3.8 million, or 0.5% sequentially, and unfavorably impacted by approximately $4.1 million, or 0.5% year-over-year.(1) Q3'26 Clear Aligner volume of 690.6 thousand cases was down 0.2% sequentially and up 6.6% year-over-year. Q3'26 Imaging Systems and CAD/CAM Services revenues were $175.5 million, down 5.3% sequentially and down 7.6% year-over-year. Q3'26 Imaging Systems and CAD/CAM Services revenues were unfavorably impacted by foreign exchange by approximately $0.9 million, or 0.5% sequentially, and unfavorably impacted by approximately $0.9 million, or 0.5% year-over-year.(1)

Q3'26 gross profit was $697.5 million, resulting in a gross margin of 68.7%. Q3'26 gross margin was unfavorably impacted by foreign exchange by approximately 0.1 points sequentially.(1) On a constant currency basis, excluding the unfavorable foreign exchange impact, Q3'26 gross margin would have been approximately 68.9%. On a year-over-year basis, Q3'26 gross margin was unfavorably impacted by foreign exchange by approximately 0.8 points.(1) On a constant currency basis, excluding the unfavorable foreign exchange impact, Q3'26 gross margin would have been approximately 69.5%. On a non-GAAP basis, Q3'26 gross profit was $714.1 million, resulting in a gross margin of 70.4%.(1)

Q3'26 operating income was $169.2 million, resulting in an operating margin of 16.7%. Q3'26 operating income was unfavorably impacted by $5.6 million accrual related to UK VAT. Q3'26 operating margin was unfavorably impacted by foreign exchange by approximately 0.2 points sequentially.(1) On a constant currency basis, excluding the unfavorable foreign exchange impact, Q3'26 operating margin would have been approximately 16.9%. On a year-over-year basis, Q3'26 operating margin was unfavorably impacted by foreign exchange by approximately 1.5 points.(1) On a constant currency basis, excluding the unfavorable foreign exchange impact, Q3'26 operating margin would have been approximately 18.2%.(1) Q3'26 net income was $135.0 million, or $1.90 per diluted share. Foreign exchange unfavorably impacted Q3'26 diluted net income per share by approximately $0.09 sequentially and by approximately $0.24 year-over-year. On a non-GAAP basis, Q3'26 net income was $198.9 million, or $2.80 per diluted share.(1)

About Non-GAAP Financial Measures

To supplement our consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP") in the United States ("U.S. GAAP"), we use the following non-GAAP financial measures: constant currency net revenues, constant currency gross profit, constant currency gross margin, constant currency income from operations, constant currency operating margin, constant currency diluted net income per share, non-GAAP constant currency gross margin, non-GAAP constant currency operating margin, non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP total operating expenses, non-GAAP income from operations, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP net income before provision for income taxes, non-GAAP provision for income taxes, non-GAAP effective tax rate, non-GAAP net income and non-GAAP diluted net income per share. These non-GAAP financial measures exclude certain items that may not be indicative of our fundamental operating performance, including foreign currency exchange rate impacts, the effects of stock-based compensation, amortization of intangible assets, restructuring and other charges, legal settlements and contingencies, acquisition-related costs, discrete cash and non-cash charges or gains and associated tax impacts that are included in the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure.

Our management believes that the use of certain non-GAAP financial measures provides meaningful supplemental information regarding our recurring core operating performance. We believe that both management and investors benefit from referring to these non-GAAP financial measures in assessing our performance and when planning, forecasting, and analyzing future periods. We believe these non-GAAP financial measures are useful to investors because (1) they allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in its financial and operational decision-making, and (2) they are used by our institutional investors and the analyst community to help them analyze the performance of our business.

There are material limitations to using non-GAAP financial measures as they are not prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP and may be different from similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies. Non-GAAP financial measures exclude certain items that may have a material impact upon our reported results of operations, which can limit their usefulness for comparison purposes. In addition, they are subject to inherent limitations as they reflect the exercise of judgments by management about which charges and gains are excluded or included from the non-GAAP financial measures. We compensate for these limitations by analyzing current and future results on both a GAAP and non-GAAP basis and by providing specific information regarding the GAAP amounts excluded or included from these non-GAAP financial measures in our public disclosures. The presentation of non-GAAP financial information is meant to be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, superior to, or in isolation from, the directly comparable financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. About Align Technology, Inc.

Align Technology designs and manufactures the Invisalign® System, the most advanced clear aligner system in the world, iTero™ intraoral scanners and services, and exocad™ CAD/CAM software. These technology building blocks enable enhanced digital orthodontic and restorative workflows to improve patient outcomes and practice efficiencies for over 307 thousand doctor customers and are key to accessing Align’s 600 million consumer market opportunity worldwide. Over the past 29 years, Align has helped doctors treat approximately 24.2 million patients with the Invisalign System and is driving the evolution in digital dentistry through the Align™ Digital Platform, our integrated suite of unique, proprietary technologies and services delivered as a seamless, end-to-end solution for patients and consumers, orthodontists and GP dentists, and lab/partners.
