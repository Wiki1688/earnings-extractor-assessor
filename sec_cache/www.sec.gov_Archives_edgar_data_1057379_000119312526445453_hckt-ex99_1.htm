The Hackett Group Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

MIAMI – November 3, 2026 – The Hackett Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: HCKT), an ROI led, AI enabled enterprise transformation company that helps organizations achieve and sustain AI World Class performance, today announced its financial results for the third quarter, which ended on September 25, 2026.

 

“We continue to build momentum as the market increasingly recognizes that meaningful value and ROI from AI transformation requires more than technology. It requires deep transformation expertise and IP to reimagine critical processes, along with AI benchmarks to accurately quantify the value opportunity. These capabilities are closely aligned with Hackett’s globally recognized transformation expertise and proprietary benchmarking and process intelligence,” stated Ted A. Fernandez, Chairman & CEO of The Hackett Group, Inc. “At the same time, the expanding use of our AI platforms, XT and AIX, across our primary enterprise transformation and software implementation offerings, is beginning to improve sales and delivery productivity. As these benefits scale across our client base, we expect them to drive increasing operating leverage and margin expansion, strengthening our confidence in our growth and profitability prospects for 2027. We have just begun to realize the benefits from our AI transition strategy.”

 

Financial Highlights

•
Total revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $69.8 million and revenue before reimbursements was $68.9 million. This compares to total revenue of $73.1 million and revenue before reimbursements of $72.2 million in the third quarter of the prior year.
•
GAAP diluted earnings per share was $0.14 in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $0.09 in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Adjusted diluted earnings per share, a non-GAAP measure, for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.39, which was at the high-end of our guidance range, as compared to $0.37 in the third quarter of 2025.
•
As of September 25, 2026, the Company’s cash balances were $12.6 million, with $86.1 million outstanding on the Company’s credit facility. Additionally, during the quarter the Company repurchased $2.5 million of its common stock and paid dividends of $3.0 million. The Company had $16.3 million available under its share repurchase plan at the end of the third quarter.
•
Subsequent to the end of the third quarter, the Company's Board of Directors declared the fourth quarterly dividend of $0.12 per share for its shareholders of record on December 24, 2026, to be paid on January 8, 2027.

 

Business Outlook for the Fourth Quarter of 2026

Based on the Company's current outlook:

•
The Company estimates total revenue before reimbursements for the fourth quarter of 2026 will be in the range of $68.0 million to $70.0 million and reflects the 6% to 8% fewer available days due to increased holidays and vacation taken when compared to the third quarter.

 

 

•
The Company estimates adjusted diluted earnings per share for the fourth quarter of 2026 to be in the range of $0.39 and $0.41, assuming a GAAP effective tax rate of 26.5%.
•
We estimate cash flow from operations to be up on a sequential basis by approximately $15.0 million to $20.0 million, with a corresponding decrease in accounts receivable of $4.0 million to $5.0 million. Consistent with our normal capital allocation strategy, this cash flow would be used to pay down debt or repurchase stock.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

The Company provides adjusted earnings results (which excludes non-cash stock based compensation expense, stock price award program compensation expense, acquisition-related cash and non-cash stock based compensation expense, amortization expense, acquisition related costs and any one-time costs and includes a GAAP tax rate) as a complement to results provided in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). These non-GAAP results are provided to enhance the users' overall understanding of the Company's current financial performance and its prospects for the future. The Company believes the non-GAAP results provide useful information to both management and investors and by excluding certain expenses that it believes are not indicative of its core operating results. The non-GAAP measures are included to provide investors and management with an alternative method for assessing operating results in a manner that is focused on the performance of its ongoing primary operations and to provide a consistent basis for comparison between quarters. Further, these non-GAAP results are one of the primary indicators management uses for planning and forecasting. The presentation of this additional non-GAAP information should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute for or superior to, any results prepared in accordance with GAAP. See the reconciliation of actual results titled "Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Measures" in the accompanying tables.

The Company believes that the presentation of non-GAAP financial information on a forward-looking basis, including the guidance contained in this release, provides important supplemental information to management and investors regarding its anticipated results of operations. The Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of GAAP measures to corresponding forward-looking non-GAAP measures without unreasonable effort due to the high variability and low visibility of most of the items that have been excluded from these non-GAAP measures. For example, non-cash stock-based compensation expense is impacted by the Company's future

 

 

hiring needs, the type and volume of equity awards necessary for such future hiring, and the price at which the Company's stock will trade in those future periods. In addition, the provision or benefit for income taxes is impacted by non-recurring income tax adjustments, valuation allowance on deferred tax assets, and the income tax effect of non-GAAP exclusions. The effects of these reconciling items may be significant, as the items that are being excluded are difficult to predict.
