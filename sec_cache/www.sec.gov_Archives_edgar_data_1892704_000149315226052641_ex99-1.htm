First Breach Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Announces Q3 2026 revenue of $569,000, exceeding the first six months of 2026

 

Unaudited October 2026 revenue to exceed $600,000

 

First Breach to host earnings call at 5:00 p.m. ET November 17, 2026

 

HAGERSTOWN, Md / ACCESS Newswire / November 16, 2026 – First Breach Inc. (NASDAQ:FBDT) (“First Breach” or the “Company”), an American-made defense technologies company focused on vertically integrated ammunition production and next-generation unmanned aerial systems, today reported financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Listing on Nasdaq was a major milestone for First Breach,” said Jeffrey Low, Chief Executive Officer of First Breach. “We are proud of what our team has accomplished. We laid out a plan to invest in people, equipment and expand production, and our progress shows that we can deliver.”

 

“The Company had a strong third quarter, generating approximately $569,000 in net revenue,” added Mr. Low. “This was approximately 57% more than we generated in the first six months of 2026 combined. Our momentum has continued growing through October 2026, with our preliminary, unaudited revenue exceeding $600,000 for the month. We continue to see growing demand for our match grade ammunition, and we are ramping production to meet it. That means hiring more people and continuing to build out our Hagerstown facility to expand ammunition production and prepare for drone manufacturing in 2027.”

 

Third Quarter Financial Results

 

●Net revenue from ammunition sales rose to $568,856 from $45,853 a year earlier. Q3 revenue was 57% above the $361,357 recorded in the first half of 2026 and nearly six times Q2 revenue of $95,353.
●Nine-month net revenue reached $930,213, exceeding full-year 2025 net revenue of $384,129.
●Gross loss narrowed to $290,426 from $651,584 a year earlier. Changes in product mix and the continued buildout of ammunition operations drove the year-over-year revenue increase.
●New equipment added, including loading and inspection systems, with expanded operating schedule and distribution.
●Noncash expenses included a one-time $10.58 million charge for broker commissions paid in common stock, part of $10.75 million in total Nasdaq direct listing costs. The Company separately recorded $8.29 million in noncash stock-based compensation.

 

 
 

 

Liquidity and Capital Resources

 

Cash was $2.17 million on September 30, 2026, compared with $2.48 million at December 31, 2025. As of September 30, 2026, First Breach had 48,076,730 shares of common stock issued and outstanding.

 

Ammunition Production and Commercial Progress

 

Manufacturing Expansion

 

The Company received and installed new loading and inspection equipment into service at its Hagerstown, Maryland facility and expanded its manufacturing schedule from five eight-hour shifts to seven 12-hour shifts, increasing scheduled operations from 40 to 84 hours per week. The workforce and facility expansion will also support the Company’s plans to begin scaling drone manufacturing in 2027.

 

First Breach is hiring to support a planned move to two 12-hour shifts per day, seven days a week and the build out of it drone manufacturing. The Company is targeting capacity of up to 20 million rounds per month as it adds staff and scales operations.

 

SAS Ammo and Preliminary October Results

 

First Breach successfully launched its three-year supply and distribution agreement with SAS Ammo in October 2026, delivering more than 3 million rounds and meeting its first monthly commitment. The agreement, announced in September, calls for monthly purchases of 3 million rounds of 5.56mm and 9mm ammunition from October through December 2026, rising to 5 million rounds per month in Q1 2027 and at least 8 million rounds per month starting in April 2027.

 

Following its first delivery month, First Breach is focused on meeting scheduled purchase volumes through the end of 2026 and into 2027 as it ramps up production.

 

Other Developments

 

Nasdaq Listing. On Aug. 20, 2026, First Breach listing and began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the symbol FBDT.

 

Drone Development. First Breach began flight testing its prototype drones in Q3 2026, completing its first untethered flight with its Attritable Close-Quarters Strike (CQS) prototype. After quarter-end, the Company completed an autonomous flight and a separate 2 km CQS test mission carrying a 500 g payload. The Long Range Strike (LRS) prototype also completed its first test flight after quarter-end, bringing both platforms into active prototype flight testing. The LRS is designed for a speed of approximately 120 km/h and a range of more than 20 km. The Company is targeting completion of its initial prototype development phase in Q4 2026 and scaled production in the second quarter of 2027, subject to testing, manufacturing readiness and available funding. As manufacturing expands, First Breach is targeting production capacity of more than 2,500 drones per week.

 

Joint venture. On October 26th, First Breach and ideaForge terminated their joint venture agreement, eliminating First Breach’s remaining capital contribution obligations of up to $25 million, including a $10 million first tranche previously scheduled for December 31, 2026. The termination allows First Breach to preserve cash and focus its resources on manufacturing its own drones in the United States.

About First Breach

 

First Breach Inc. is an ISO 9001:2015 certified, American-made defense technologies company focused on manufacturing match-grade ammunition components, finished ammunition, and developing next-generation unmanned aerial systems for commercial, law enforcement, and military markets. The Company manufactures its products in-house at its Hagerstown, Maryland facility, where it produces brass cups, casings, projectiles, lead cores, lead wire, and completed ammunition with rigorous quality control standards. First Breach is also advancing its drone strategy through the development of U.S.-made unmanned systems, leveraging advanced engineering, robotics, ISR and sensor technologies, and precision manufacturing capabilities to address evolving defense, homeland security, law enforcement, and commercial requirements across domestic and international markets.
