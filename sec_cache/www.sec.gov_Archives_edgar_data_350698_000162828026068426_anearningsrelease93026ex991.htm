AutoNation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
•Q3 2026 EPS $4.87 and Adjusted EPS $5.11, seventh consecutive quarter of YoY growth
•Strong cash flow generation and cash conversion
•Share repurchases of $620 million year-to-date, 9% reduction of shares outstanding
•Additional $1 billion of share repurchase authorization approved
•Record After-Sales gross profit; Customer Pay growth 6%
•AN Finance portfolio growth of more than 40% YoY, profit up 10X YoY

FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., (October 30, 2026) — AutoNation, Inc. (NYSE: AN) today reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $7.0 billion, relatively flat compared to the same period a year ago. Third quarter 2026 EPS was $4.87, compared to $5.65 a year ago, and third quarter 2026 Adjusted EPS was $5.11, compared to $5.01 a year ago. Reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures are included in the attached financial tables.

“Our team delivered another solid quarter, growing adjusted EPS for the seventh consecutive quarter,” said Mike Manley, Chief Executive Officer of AutoNation. “Record After-Sales performance and strong growth in AutoNation Finance further increased the contribution of recurring earnings and cash flow, leading to exceptional cash generation and disciplined capital allocation. We utilized nearly 85 percent of our adjusted free cash flow for share repurchases, reducing shares outstanding by 9 percent year-to-date. The current environment strongly favors allocating capital toward share repurchases and as such we have secured Board authorization for an additional $1 billion in share repurchases,” Manley concluded.

Capital Allocation, Liquidity, and Leverage
For the first nine months ended September 30, 2026, cash used in operating activities was $160.4 million, auto loans receivable, net, increased $708.4 million, capital expenditures were $201.7 million, and adjusted free cash flow was $739.0 million, or 141% of adjusted net income.

During the first nine months of 2026, AutoNation repurchased 3.1 million shares for an aggregate purchase price of $620 million or $198.68 per share. Year-to-date through October 28, 2026, AutoNation repurchased 3.4 million shares for an aggregate purchase price of $668 million or $194.99 per share. In October 2026, AutoNation’s Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of up to an additional $1.0 billion of common stock under its share repurchase program. As of October 28, 2026, $1.4 billion remained available for share repurchases under the program.

As of September 30, 2026, AutoNation had $1.5 billion of liquidity, including $51 million in cash and $1.4 billion of availability under its revolving credit facility, net of commercial paper borrowings. The Company’s covenant leverage ratio was 2.7X at quarter-end, and the Company had $4.3 billion of non-vehicle debt outstanding.



In September 2026, AutoNation amended its unsecured Credit Agreement, increasing the revolving borrowing capacity to $2.0 billion and extending the maturity date to September 14, 2031, among other things. In October 2026, the Company increased its commercial paper program capacity to $2.0 billion, matching the capacity under its revolving credit facility.

About AutoNation, Inc.
AutoNation, one of the largest automotive retailers in the United States, offers innovative products and exceptional services as part of a portfolio of comprehensive solutions for our customers and their automotive needs. With a nationwide network of dealerships strengthened by a recognized brand, we offer a wide variety of new and used vehicles, customer financing, parts, and expert maintenance and repair services. Through DRV PNK, we have raised over $50 million for cancer-related causes, demonstrating our commitment to making a positive difference in the lives of our Associates, Customers, and the communities we serve.
