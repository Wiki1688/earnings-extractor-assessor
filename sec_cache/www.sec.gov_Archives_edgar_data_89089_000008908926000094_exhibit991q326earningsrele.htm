SERVICE CORPORATION INTERNATIONAL
ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
AND UPDATES FOURTH QUARTER OUTLOOK
Conference call on Thursday, October 29, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. Central Time.
HOUSTON, Texas, October 28, 2026 . . . Service Corporation International (NYSE: SCI), the largest provider of deathcare products and services in North America, today reported results for the third quarter of 2026.
Third Quarter Highlights:
–GAAP earnings per share increased 24% to $1.03 from $0.83 in the third quarter of 2025
–Adjusted earnings per share increased 11% to $0.97 from $0.87 in the third quarter of 2025
–Consolidated revenue grew $49.2 million, or 5%, over the third quarter of 2025
–Comparable funeral preneed sales production increased 6% in the current quarter
–Comparable cemetery preneed sales production increased 5% in the current quarter
–Comparable total funeral sales average grew 3% over the third quarter of 2025
–Net cash provided by operating activities increased $44.5 million, or 18%, to $296.8 million in the current quarter compared to $252.3 million in the prior-year quarter
–Adjusted cash provided by operating activities increased $29.0 million, or 11%, to $296.9 million in the current quarter compared to $267.9 million in the prior year
Tom Ryan, the Company's Chairman and CEO, commented on the third quarter performance:
"Today, we reported adjusted earnings per share of $0.97 and adjusted net cash provided by operating activities of $296.9 million, both delivering double-digit growth compared to the prior-year quarter. Our funeral segment delivered solid results, supported by continued growth in average revenue per service and stable funeral service volumes. Our cemetery segment generated 4% growth in comparable revenue, driven by higher recognized preneed property, merchandise and service revenue.
Preneed sales production remained a key contributor to our long-term growth outlook, with comparable funeral preneed sales production increasing 6% and comparable cemetery preneed sales production growing 5%. Additionally, recognized preneed cemetery property revenue grew 1%, while preneed property sales production increased 4%. This dynamic puts near-term pressure on cemetery gross margins but continues to expand our backlog with higher-margin deferred property sales, which will benefit us in future periods.
Despite these positive revenue and sales trends, margins were pressured by higher healthcare costs, higher selling compensation costs and increased advertising and marketing expenditures supporting our preneed sales growth. While we expect these elevated costs to continue into the fourth quarter, as reflected in our earnings outlook, our investments in preneed sales production will drive revenue and earnings growth as sales are recognized in the future. The impact of these higher costs during the quarter was partially offset by higher trust fund income and lower corporate general and administrative expenses, primarily reflecting reduced long-term incentive compensation tied to total shareholder return.
We continue to actively deploy capital across a range of strategic growth opportunities, including acquisitions, cemetery property development, real estate investments, and new funeral home construction. Our consistent cash flow generation allows us to fund these investments to support future growth while continuing to return significant capital to shareholders. Year to date, we have returned $464 million through dividends and share repurchases, reflecting our disciplined approach to creating long-term shareholder value.
Our results reflect the dedication of our more than 25,000 associates who make a difference in the lives of families every day. I am grateful for their unwavering commitment to providing exceptional care and service to the families and communities we serve.”

UPDATED FOURTH QUARTER 2026 EARNINGS OUTLOOK; CASH FLOW CONFIRMED
We expect fourth quarter 2026 adjusted earnings per share of $1.10 to $1.20. Full year adjusted earnings per share are now expected to be $3.94 to $4.04. While we expect year-over-year revenue growth, this outlook reflects lower funeral volumes than we originally anticipated, coupled with the continued impact of higher healthcare costs, as well as higher selling compensation and advertising and marketing expenditures supporting our growth in preneed sales production. While these selling costs are impacting near-term earnings, these investments in preneed sales will contribute to future revenue and earnings growth as the related sales are recognized.

We are maintaining our full-year adjusted operating cash flow guidance, with a midpoint of $1,085 million, as well as our maintenance capital expenditure outlook of $335 million, resulting in expected adjusted free cash flow of approximately $750 million.

ABOUT SERVICE CORPORATION INTERNATIONAL
Service Corporation International (NYSE: SCI), headquartered in Houston, Texas, is North America's leading provider of funeral, cemetery and cremation services, as well as final-arrangement planning in advance, serving approximately 700,000 combined preneed and atneed families each year. Our diversified portfolio of brands provides families and individuals a full range of choices to meet their needs, from simple cremations to full life celebrations and personalized remembrances. Our Dignity Memorial® brand is the name families turn to for professionalism, compassion, and attention to detail that is second to none. At September 30, 2026, we owned and operated 1,491 funeral service locations and 506 cemeteries (of which 317 are combination locations) in 44 states, eight Canadian provinces, the District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico. For more information about Service Corporation International, please visit our website at www.sci-corp.com. For more information about Dignity Memorial®, please visit www.dignitymemorial.com.

•Total comparable funeral revenue increased $18.7 million. Core funeral revenue increased $10.6 million, or 2.3%, primarily due to a 2.8% increase in core average revenue per service partially offset by a 0.5% decrease in core funeral services performed. The core cremation rate increased 60 basis points to 58.2%.
•Non-funeral home revenue increased $2.7 million, or 10.6%, due to an 8.4% increase in non-funeral home average revenue per service driven by higher matured preneed revenue from the backlog, combined with a 2.0% increase in non-funeral home services performed.
•Non-funeral home preneed sales revenue decreased $1.7 million, primarily due to a reduction in general agency revenue. While non-funeral home insurance sales production increased 9.3%, a lower general agency commission rate impacted revenue quarter over quarter.
•Core general agency and other revenue increased $7.1 million. Core general agency revenue benefited from higher insurance sales production partially offset by a slightly lower general agency commission rate quarter over quarter. The current commission rate is generally stable and is trending in line with expectations.
•Comparable funeral gross profit increased $3.6 million to $103.8 million, and the gross profit percentage was 17.6% in both periods. Comparable funeral revenue increased $18.7 million which was partially offset by higher selling compensation and higher advertising and marketing expenditures supporting preneed sales production. In addition, we experienced an increase in health insurance expense.
•Comparable funeral preneed sales production increased $18.6 million, or 6.1%, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 2025, driven by strong increases in core and non-funeral home preneed sales production.

•Total comparable cemetery revenue increased $19.4 million, or 4.0%, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase was due to higher core revenue of $11.9 million and higher other revenue of $7.5 million.
•Core revenue increased $11.9 million, primarily driven by a $13.6 million, or 4.0%, increase in total recognized preneed revenue from higher property and merchandise and service revenue. This includes the benefit of increased trust fund income. Additionally, recognized preneed cemetery property revenue grew 1.2%, while preneed property sales production increased 4.2%. This dynamic puts near-term pressure on cemetery gross margins but builds the backlog of revenue to be recognized in future periods.
•Other revenue was $7.5 million higher, or 19.7%, compared to the prior-year quarter primarily due to an increase in endowment care trust fund income based on market performance and higher total return distributions.
•Comparable cemetery gross profit increased $1.1 million to $167.1 million. The gross profit percentage decreased from 34.3% to 33.2%. Comparable cemetery revenue increased $19.4 million, which was largely offset by higher selling compensation and higher advertising and marketing expenditures supporting preneed sales production. Changes to our sales compensation structure, which increased guaranteed hourly wages, resulted in a higher selling compensation rate. In addition, this resulted in more selling costs being recognized in the current period rather than deferred, which will reduce selling compensation expense recognized in subsequent periods. We also experienced an increase in health insurance expense. While these elevated costs are pressuring cemetery gross margins, the growth in preneed sales production continues to strengthen our backlog with quality deferred property, merchandise and service revenue, which will benefit future periods.
•Comparable preneed cemetery sales production increased $17.0 million, or 4.8%, and was supported by a combination of increases in the number of contracts sold, higher sales averages, and a $3.7 million increase in large sales.
Other Financial Results
•Corporate general and administrative expenses were $30.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $38.3 million in the prior year. The decrease was driven primarily by lower long-term incentive compensation expense tied to total shareholder return and a favorable legal settlement.
•Interest expense was $65.0 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $65.7 million in the prior year, resulting in a $0.7 million decrease due to lower rates on floating rate debt more than offsetting higher balances.
•Gains (losses) on divestitures and impairment charges, net increased to $11.2 million in the third quarter of 2026 due to non-strategic asset divestitures of excess land compared to a $0.4 million net pre-tax loss in the third quarter of 2025.
•The GAAP effective income tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 25.1%, down from 26.9% in the prior-year quarter. On an adjusted basis, the effective tax rate was 25.3%, up from 24.1% in the prior-year quarter. The higher GAAP effective tax rate in the prior period was primarily due to a change in estimate recorded in the prior year related to the finalization of the 2024 tax return. The higher adjusted effective tax rate in the current year is primarily due to lower non-taxable gains in the cash surrender value of certain life insurance policies from lower returns in the financial markets year over year.
