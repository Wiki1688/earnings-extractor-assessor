News Release
CMCO's Growth and Integration Momentum Continues in Q2 FY27; Increases Full Year Guidance
•Kito Crosby integration remains on track, with synergy realization ahead of plan
•Net sales growth of 107% Y/Y driven by the Kito Crosby Acquisition with solid growth at both Legacy CMCO1 and Kito Crosby
•Book-to-bill of 1.0x reflects strong conversion of orders to revenue and positions CMCO for continued progress in 2H FY27
•Increases FY27 guidance for Net sales, Adjusted EBITDA2 and Adjusted EPS2
CHARLOTTE, NC, November 3, 2026 - Columbus McKinnon Corporation (Nasdaq: CMCO) ("CMCO" or the "Company"), today announced financial results for its fiscal year 2027 second quarter, which ended September 30, 2026.
The Company delivered net sales growth of 107% and order growth of 122% year-over-year, both driven by the acquisition of Kito Crosby Limited ("Kito Crosby"), which closed on February 3, 2026 (the "Kito Crosby Acquisition"), coupled with strong performance across the Legacy CMCO1 and Kito Crosby platforms.
“Building on record orders and sales in the first quarter, our team delivered strong second quarter performance that exceeded expectations. Sequential revenue growth was supported by sustained strength in the Americas and bolstered by stronger than anticipated demand in both Asia and EMEA," said David J. Wilson, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Our continued focus on improving operational execution and customer experience led to increased demand from both project and short-cycle business. Early success and strong customer response to our combined portfolio reinforce our confidence in the growth opportunities created by bringing CMCO and Kito Crosby together.”
Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Highlights (compared with prior-year period, except where otherwise noted)
•Net sales of $539.3 million increased 107%
•Orders of $562.6 million increased 122%; Book-to-bill of 1.0x
•Net loss attributable to the Company of $25.5 million, or $0.76 per diluted share reflects $12.5 million of acquisition and integration related expenses
•Adjusted Net Income2 of $31.1 million increased 74% and Adjusted EPS2,4 of $0.61 declined $0.01 largely due to a higher share count and increased interest expense
•Adjusted EBITDA2 of $111.2 million and Adjusted EBITDA Margin2 of 20.6%, up 520 basis points
“Our integration of Kito Crosby is progressing well, supported by agile execution and strong collaboration across the combined organization. With our initial integration priorities firmly underway, we are sharpening our focus on the longer-term initiatives that will unlock the potential of the combined business," continued Wilson. "Our teams are working effectively as one, and the meaningful progress we are making provides higher confidence in achieving our targeted value creation objectives."

Capital, Liquidity and Cash Flow
The Company ended the second quarter with a Credit Agreement Net Leverage Ratio2 of 4.9x and total liquidity of $532.0 million consisting of $110.9 million of cash and cash equivalents, $421.1 million of availability on the Company's revolving credit facility.
The Company has generated $21.1 million in cash provided by operating activities year-to-date, with capital expenditures of $16.5 million resulting in Free Cash Flow2 of $4.6 million. Year-to-date cash costs related to the Kito Crosby Acquisition were $24.3 million.
The Company remains committed to allocating capital to pay down debt to de-lever its balance sheet in the near term while continuing its track record of consistent dividend payments.

About Columbus McKinnon Corporation
CMCO is a global leader in intelligent motion solutions designed to advance performance and productivity, helping customers move the world forward with confidence. Guided by its mission to deliver innovative solutions with unmatched safety, quality and reliability, CMCO enables efficient lifting, positioning, securing and movement of materials across a wide range of end markets. Its portfolio spans five key platforms: lifting hardware consumables, hoists and cranes, precision conveyance, automation and linear motion. Driven by a vision for a safer, more productive tomorrow, CMCO partners with customers to solve some of their most complex intralogistics challenges and keep industry in motion. Comprehensive information is available at www.cmco.com.
