SAN FRANCISCO, Calif. – (BUSINESS WIRE) – October 27, 2026 – SoFi Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ: SOFI), a member-centric, everything app for digital financial services that helps members borrow, save, spend, invest and protect their money, reported financial results today for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“We had exceptional Q3 results against the backdrop of any environment, but they are even more remarkable given how 2026 has unfolded,” said Anthony Noto, CEO of SoFi. “Our focus is simple. We are adding more members, who are using more products, and generating more revenue per product to create the most value for our shareholders over the long run. In less than a decade, we have transformed SoFi from a single-product company into an everything financial app with eight different businesses each generating over $100 million in annualized revenue. Our conviction in the opportunity ahead has never been greater.”

Highlights
•Raising Guidance on Continued Business Momentum. Given the strength and visibility across the business, SoFi raised its 2026 guidance above analyst estimates1, reflecting both the third quarter beat and higher expectations for the fourth quarter of 2026. SoFi now expects $5.04 billion of adjusted net revenue which is a year-over-year increase of 40%, $1.66 billion of adjusted EBITDA, $840.0 million of net income and $0.61 of EPS. The company also introduced 2027 guidance of approximately $6.4 billion adjusted net revenue, $2.24 billion of adjusted EBITDA, $1.28 billion of net income and $0.90 of EPS, all of which are

•Delivering Record Results with Durable Growth and Strong Returns. Adjusted net revenue increased 44% year-over-year to $1.4 billion, while adjusted EBITDA increased 70% to $469.8 million, representing a 34% margin. SoFi achieved the Rule of 40 for the 20th consecutive quarter, with a score of 79. SoFi also delivered its 12th consecutive profitable quarter with adjusted net income of $243.0 million, and a return on tangible common equity of approximately 10%. Fee-based revenue reached approximately $570.6 million, representing 42% of total revenue.
•Growing Members, Products and Revenue Per Product. SoFi grew members 35% year-over-year to 17.1 million, adding a record 1.2 million members during the quarter, while products increased 44% to 26.7 million, including a record 2.2 million products added. Products per member increased to approximately 1.56 from 1.47 a year ago and 51% of new products were opened by existing members. This continued growth reflects the strength of SoFi’s Financial Services Productivity Loop (FSPL) strategy, as members use more products, relationships deepen and the opportunity to generate revenue from each relationship expands.
•Achieving Record Loan Originations While Maintaining Strong Credit Performance. Total loan originations reached a record $16.9 billion, including approximately $3.8 billion of Loan Platform Business originations. Lending adjusted net revenue reached approximately $807.4 million, while Lending products increased 39% year-over-year to 3.4 million, and credit performance remained strong and in line with expectations. Personal Loan products reached approximately 2.6 million, Student Loan products reached approximately 760,000 and Home Loan products reached approximately 72,000. Lending adjusted revenue per product increased to approximately $942, compared with approximately $920 in the second quarter of 2026 and $782 in the third quarter of 2025.
•Driving Financial Services Revenue Through Product Growth and Monetization. Financial Services revenue increased 25% year-over-year to approximately $525.1 million, while products grew 44% to 23.2 million. Money and Relay drove the greatest absolute growth, each surpassing 8 million products and increasing 34% and 45%, respectively. Invest grew 37% to more than 4 million products, while Credit Card grew 67% to approximately 656,000 products, with a high percentage of primary card users and strong credit performance. Annualized Financial Services revenue per product increased to approximately $90 in the third quarter from $88 in the second quarter of 2026, reflecting continued monetization through deposits, spending, brokerage, lending referrals, subscriptions, credit card balances and other fee-based services.
•Setting Bold Ambitions for 2030. SoFi now has eight businesses that each generated over $100 million in annualized revenue. By 2030, the Company plans on reaching 100 million products and scaling five businesses to more than $1 billion in revenue, five businesses to more than $500 million in revenue and five businesses to more than $100 million in revenue. This reflects SoFi's strategy to evolve from a single-product student loan refinancing business in 2018 to an everything financial app serving 17.1 million members today.

For the third quarter of 2026, record origination volume of $16.9 billion increased 71% year-over-year. This was a result of continued strong member demand for personal loans, student loans and home loans as well as strong demand from capital markets partners.
Record personal loan originations of $12.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026 were up 69% year-over-year, inclusive of $3.6 billion originated on behalf of third parties through our Loan Platform Business. SoFi's multichannel strategy continues to allow us to serve more members and provide revenue diversification.
Third quarter student loan volume of $2.9 billion was up 96% year-over-year. This marked the highest quarter of student loan originations in SoFi's history.
Home loan volume was $1.4 billion, an increase of 44% year-over-year. Home equity loan originations were strong during the third quarter, accounting for approximately 40% of total home loan volume.
Capital markets activity in the third quarter of 2026 was strong. Overall, SoFi sold, or transferred through our Loan Platform Business, more than $5.3 billion in total of personal loans, home loans and small business loans. In terms of home loan sales, we closed $1.4 billion at a blended execution of 103.6%.
During the quarter, SoFi executed one co-contributor securitization of loans previously originated through our Loan Platform Business, totaling $460 million. This marked the eighth securitization of new collateral under our SoFi Consumer Loan Program (SCLP) since 2021 using collateral originated in the Loan Platform Business. Importantly, this channel provides our partners with meaningful liquidity to support their ongoing investment in the Loan Platform Business. The transaction priced at industry-leading cost-of-funds levels, with a weighted average spread of 83 basis points.
Credit performance for personal loans remained strong in the third quarter, in line with expectations. Excluding the impact of late stage delinquent loan sales, it is estimated that, including recoveries, the all-in annualized net charge-off rate for personal loans would have been approximately 3.4%, a 30 basis point improvement from the prior quarter and an 80 basis point improvement from the prior year period, driven by an improvement in the underlying performance as well as strong growth in average loans.
The personal loan annualized charge-off rate decreased 34 basis points year-over-year to 2.26%, which includes the impact of asset sales, new originations and delinquency sales in the quarter. The student loan annualized charge-off rate increased to 63 basis points from 61 basis points in the prior quarter, and decreased from 69 basis points in the prior-year period.
The on-balance sheet 90-day delinquency rates for both personal loans and student loans were relatively consistent with the prior year.
The data continues to support a 7–8% maximum cumulative net loss assumption for personal loans, in line with SoFi's underwriting tolerance.
Recent vintages, originated from the fourth quarter of 2022 to fourth quarter of 2025 have net cumulative losses of 4.71%, with 33% unpaid principal balance remaining. This is well below the 6.62% observed at the same point in

Members
We refer to our customers as “members”. We define a member as someone who has a lending relationship with us through origination and/or ongoing servicing, opened a financial services account, linked an external account to our platform, or signed up for our credit score monitoring service. Our members have access to our CFPs, our member events, our content, educational material, news, and our tools and calculators, which are provided at no cost to the member. We view members as an indication not only of the size and a measurement of growth of our business, but also as a measure of the significant value of the data we have collected over time.
Once someone becomes a member, they are always considered a member unless they are removed in accordance with our terms of service, in which case, we adjust our total number of members. This could occur for a variety of reasons—including fraud or pursuant to certain legal processes—and, as our terms of service evolve together with our business practices, product offerings and applicable regulations, our grounds for removing members from our total member count could change. The determination that a member should be removed in accordance with our terms of service is subject to an evaluation process, following the completion, and based on the results, of which, relevant members and their associated products are removed from our total member count in the period in which such evaluation process concludes. However, depending on the length of the evaluation process, that removal may not take place in the same period in which the member was added to our member count or the same period in which the circumstances leading to their removal occurred. For this reason, our total member count may not yet reflect adjustments that may be made once ongoing evaluation processes, if any, conclude. Beginning in the first quarter of 2024, we aligned our methodology for calculating member and product metrics with our member and product definitions to include co-borrowers, co-signers, and joint- and co-account holders, as applicable. Total Products
Total products refers to the aggregate number of lending and financial services products that our members have selected on our platform since our inception through the reporting date, whether or not the members are still registered for such products. Total products is a primary indicator of the size and reach of our Lending and Financial Services segments. Management relies on total products metrics to understand the effectiveness of our member acquisition efforts and to gauge the propensity for members to use more than one product.
In our Lending segment, total products refers to the number of personal loans, student loans and home loans that have been originated through our platform through the reporting date, inclusive of loans which we originate as part of our Loan Platform Business, whether or not such loans have been paid off. If a member has multiple loan products of the same loan product type, such as two personal loans, that is counted as a single product. However, if a member has multiple loan products across loan product types, such as one personal loan and one home loan, that is counted as two products. The account of a co-borrower or co-signer is not considered a separate lending product.
In our Financial Services segment, total products refers to the number of SoFi Money accounts (inclusive of checking and savings accounts held at SoFi Bank and cash management accounts), SoFi Invest accounts, SoFi Credit Card accounts (including Smart Card accounts, credit card with personal loan rollover loans and accounts with a zero dollar balance at the reporting date), referred loans (which are originated by a third-party partner to which we provide pre-qualified borrower referrals), SoFi At Work accounts, SoFi Relay accounts (with either credit score monitoring enabled or external linked accounts), and SoFi Crypto accounts that have been opened through our platform through the reporting date, as well as active SoFi Plus subscriptions as of the reporting date.
