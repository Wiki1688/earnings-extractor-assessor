JBT Marel Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Announces Increased Share Repurchase Authorization

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:
◦Achieved net income margin of 7.0 percent, and adjusted EBITDA margin of 18.0 percent, both of which were record quarterly levels
◦Utilized strong cash flow to repurchase approximately 800,000 shares for $94 million
◦Delivered diluted earnings per share of $1.34, and adjusted earnings per share of $2.19
◦Announcing increased share repurchase program, with full program now totaling $700 million

CHICAGO, November 3, 2026 - JBT Marel Corporation (NYSE and Nasdaq Iceland: JBTM), a leading global technology solutions provider to high-value segments of the food & beverage industry, today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026.

"We executed well in a dynamic environment, delivering third quarter adjusted EBITDA and margins above the midpoint of our guidance, notwithstanding lower book-and-ship volume," said Brian Deck, Chief Executive Officer. "Our strong quarterly and year-to-date margins reflect the meaningful benefits from our cost synergy initiatives, including labor productivity, supply chain savings, and footprint optimization. The commitment to our margin improvement program will continue to drive results even amid inflationary and tariff pressures."

“We are revising our full-year 2026 revenue outlook to reflect lower, near-term CPG-related volume expectations, and at the same time, are increasing the midpoint of our margin guidance as we continue to drive operational efficiencies and disciplined cost management," added Deck.

Increased Share Repurchase Authorization

On October 30, 2026, JBT Marel's Board of Directors authorized a $500 million increase to the existing share repurchase program. The full share repurchase authorization now totals $700 million. The Company expects any share repurchases to be balanced to maintain a leverage ratio within the desired long-term range of 2.0 - 2.5x.










JBT Marel Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results

“We utilized our strong third quarter cash flow to repurchase approximately 800,000 shares and return $94 million to shareholders,” said Matt Meister, Chief Financial Officer. “The increased share purchase program announced today provides greater flexibility to opportunistically repurchase shares when we believe our stock is trading below the long-term value of JBT Marel. We view share repurchases as an important component of our disciplined capital allocation strategy and a compelling use of capital when the valuation warrants."

Third quarter 2026 consolidated revenue of $994 million declined 1 percent. Revenue was below Company expectations due to lower CPG-related book-and-ship business within the Prepared Food and Beverage Solutions segment. Net income of $70 million increased 6 percent, and net income margin of 7.0 percent improved 30 basis points. Foreign exchange translation had a minimal impact on results.

Third quarter 2026 consolidated adjusted EBITDA of $179 million increased 5 percent, and adjusted EBITDA margin of 18.0 percent improved 90 basis points. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $1.34 compared to $1.26. Adjusted EPS was $2.19 compared to $1.94. Orders totaled $976 million, and quarter-ending backlog was $1.52 billion.

Year to date 2026 operating cash flow was $320 million, and free cash flow was $252 million. As of September 30, 2026, the Company's net debt to trailing twelve months adjusted EBITDA was 2.45x after returning $94 million to shareholders through share repurchases.

Third quarter 2026 Protein Solutions segment revenue increased 6 percent, primarily driven by volume growth in the Poultry business. Segment adjusted EBITDA margin improved 110 basis points, primarily driven by the benefits of volume leverage, pricing, and cost synergy realization. Foreign exchange translation had a minimal impact on segment results.

Third quarter 2026 Prepared Food and Beverage Solutions segment revenue declined 6 percent, primarily reflecting lower revenue within the Diversified Food and Health business due to softer CPG-related book-and-ship volume. Segment adjusted EBITDA margin improved 90 basis points as benefits from restructuring within the warehouse automation business along with overall segment mix, cost synergy realization, and pricing more than offset lower volume leverage impacts. Sequentially, adjusted EBITDA margin improved 170 basis points. Foreign exchange translation had a minimal impact on segment results.

About JBT Marel Corporation

JBT Marel Corporation (NYSE and Nasdaq Iceland: JBTM) is a leading global technology solutions provider to high-value segments of the food & beverage industry. JBT Marel’s unique solutions of integrated equipment, service, software, and application expertise enables customers to optimize food yield and efficiency, improve food safety and quality, and enhance uptime and proactive maintenance, all while reducing waste and resource use across the global food supply chain. JBT Marel operates more


than 50 manufacturing and distribution facilities globally. For more information, please visit www.jbtmarel.com.

Non-GAAP Measures and Reconciliations to GAAP Measures

Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted income, Adjusted diluted earnings per share (“Adjusted EPS”), and Free cash flow are non-GAAP financial measures. JBT Marel provides non-GAAP financial measures in order to increase transparency in our operating results and trends. These non-GAAP measures eliminate certain costs or benefits from, or change the calculation of, a measure as calculated under U.S. GAAP. By eliminating these items, JBT Marel provides a more meaningful comparison of our ongoing operating results, consistent with how management evaluates performance. Management uses these non-GAAP measures in financial and operational evaluation, planning and forecasting. These calculations may differ from similarly-titled measures used by other companies. The non-GAAP financial measures disclosed are not intended to be used as a substitute for, nor should they be considered in isolation of, financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures can be found in the supplemental schedules to this release.
