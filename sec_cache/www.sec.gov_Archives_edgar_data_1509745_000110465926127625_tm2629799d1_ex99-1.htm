Cypherpunk Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

First quarter of Zcash Mining: 8,967.29 ZEC Mined in 44 days, representing $9.9 million revenue at approximately $104 cost of mining revenue per ZEC and 95% uptime

 

Treasury increased to 341,858.75 ZEC, including October mining results and recent open market purchases, approximately 2.01% of circulating supply

 

Company to host X Spaces event today, November 16, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. ET

 

CAMBRIDGE, Mass. – November 16, 2026 – Cypherpunk Technologies Inc. (Nasdaq: CYPH) ("Cypherpunk"), a privacy technology company, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“The launch of Cypherpunk Mining in August was transformative, as it enabled the production of ZEC alongside our purchases and contributes to the security of the Zcash network. In the first six weeks of operation, our fleet earned 8,967.29 ZEC as a result of an exceptional 95% uptime, all of which was added to our treasury in a momentous third quarter for the company,” said Douglas E. Onsi, President and Chief Executive Officer of Cypherpunk Technologies. “As we hold the ZEC we mine, we translated our Zcash flow into $9.9 million in recorded revenue. The average fair value of the mined ZEC was more than $1,100, while our hosting cost to mine one ZEC was approximately $104.”

 

“The quarter marked a major milestone for Cypherpunk, and we are already planning for significant continued growth. We intend to increase our overall hashrate through additional equipment purchases with the goal of representing approximately 25% of the total Zcash network over time,” said Will McEvoy, Chief Investment Officer of Cypherpunk. “Cypherpunk Mining is the newest piece of the constellation of privacy technologies and investments that we are assembling. With 341,858.75 ZEC held, representing 2.01% of the total circulating supply, we continue to execute on our objective of holding at least 5% of the ZEC supply over time. We also plan to expand our efforts in privacy technology development and investment more broadly as we head into 2027.”

 

Cypherpunk Highlights:

 

Launched Cypherpunk Mining, the world's largest Zcash mining fleet at launch

 

·On August 17, 2026, Cypherpunk Mining acquired from Moria Mining, LLC, an affiliate of Winklevoss Treasury Investments, LLC ("WTI"), Bitmain Antminer Z15 Pro Zcash mining machines, the colocation and hosting agreements under which those machines are installed and operated at three third-party hosting facilities, and related hosting deposits (the "Mining Asset Acquisition"). The purchase price of $33.33 million was paid through the issuance to WTI of a pre-funded warrant to purchase 43,290,042 shares of Cypherpunk common stock at an exercise price of $0.001 per share.

 

·Cypherpunk's mining fleet launched on August 18, 2026, and at the time was the largest Zcash mining operation in the world. Cypherpunk's fleet has a deployed hashrate of approximately 4.2 GSol/s. At the closing of the Mining Asset Acquisition, Cypherpunk's fleet represented approximately 18% of total Zcash network hashrate. Cypherpunk's mining business is led by Kevin Zhang, its Head of Mining, who joined the Company in August 2026.

 

 

 

 

Key operating metrics

 

·Mining revenue was $9.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, Cypherpunk's first period of mining operations, and reflects 8,967.29 ZEC earned from August 18, 2026 through September 30, 2026, at an average fair value of $1,105.46 per ZEC.

 

·Fleet operations demonstrated approximately 95% uptime.

 

·Total Zcash network hashrate increased from approximately 23 GSol/s at August 18, 2026 to approximately 32 GSol/s as of November 13, 2026. Cypherpunk's share of network hashrate represents approximately 13.1% as of November 13, 2026.

 

·Cost to mine one ZEC, excluding depreciation of mining equipment, was $104.33, or 9.44% of the production value of one ZEC mined. Cost to mine one ZEC, including depreciation of mining equipment, was $237.46, or 21.48% of the production value of one ZEC mined.

 

Positioning the fleet to grow with the network

 

·To expand the mining fleet, in September 2026, Cypherpunk Mining entered into a letter of intent with Bitmain Technologies Delaware Limited ("Bitmain") for the purchase of Bitmain's next-generation Zcash mining machines with a total purchase commitment of up to $30.0 million, of which $6.0 million was paid as a refundable deposit, with delivery expected in mid-2027. Completion of this transaction would expand Cypherpunk's mining fleet towards the goal of representing approximately 25% of the overall Zcash network hashrate as that hashrate increases over the next year.

 

October mining results of 5,721.35 ZEC

 

·In October 2026, Cypherpunk mined an additional 5,721.35 ZEC, all of which were added to the treasury, and achieved an uptime of approximately 97%.

 

ZEC treasury holdings increased to 341,858.75 ZEC

 

·Since the end of the third quarter, Cypherpunk acquired an additional 3,775.72 ZEC through open market purchases for approximately $3.475 million at an average price of $920.40 per ZEC.

 

·Including the recent open market purchases and the October mining results, Cypherpunk holds a total of 341,858.75 ZEC, representing approximately 2.01% of the circulating supply of ZEC. Cypherpunk's current stated objective is to accumulate holdings representing at least 5% of the circulating supply of ZEC over time.

 

Amanda Fabiano appointed to Board of Directors

 

·In September 2026, Cypherpunk appointed Amanda Fabiano to its Board of Directors. Fabiano currently serves as Chief Operating Officer of Nakamoto Inc., a publicly listed bitcoin-focused company, and as an independent director of TeraWulf Inc., a public bitcoin mining company that has expanded into AI and high-performance computing infrastructure. Previously, she held senior mining and investment roles at Galaxy and Fidelity.

 

Leap Therapeutics Subsidiary Highlights:

 

·Following publication of the randomized Phase 2 DeFianCe study in Clinical Cancer Research in August 2026, receipt of Fast Track designation in May 2026, and alignment with the FDA on the design of a registrational Phase 3 trial in a DKK1-high 2L CRC population, Leap initiated a strategic process to identify the best path forward for sirexatamab and to secure the resources required to advance the program into Phase 3 development. The process may include financing the program as an independent entity or a strategic transaction with a pharmaceutical or biotechnology company, including a partnership, license, collaboration, sale or other business combination. There can be no assurance that the process will result in any transaction or financing, and the Company has not set a timetable for its conclusion.

 

 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:

 

Cypherpunk recorded net income of $306.0 million, or $1.44 per basic share and $1.13 per diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $3.3 million, or $0.08 per basic and diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Cypherpunk's net income for the 2026 period was primarily driven by the non-cash unrealized gain on the embedded derivative, reflecting the increase in the price of ZEC from $400.09 per ZEC at June 30, 2026 to $1,414.56 per ZEC at September 30, 2026, partially offset by a provision for income taxes of $27.5 million.

 

Mining revenue was $9.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, Cypherpunk's first period of mining operations, and reflects 8,967.29 ZEC earned from August 18, 2026 through September 30, 2026 at an average fair value of $1,105.46 per ZEC. Cypherpunk had no revenue in the three months ended September 30, 2025. Mining revenue is a non-cash item, as Cypherpunk received ZEC, not cash.

 

Cost of revenue (excluding depreciation and amortization) was $0.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and consisted of hosting costs, including electricity pass-through charges.

 

Depreciation expense related to Cypherpunk's mining equipment was $1.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, representing 44 days of depreciation of the mining machines that were acquired in the Mining Asset Acquisition on a straight-line basis over an estimated useful life of three (3) years.

 

Research and development expenses were $0.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease of $1.0 million in research and development expenses during the three months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to the same period in 2025, was primarily due to a decrease in headcount, clinical trial, and manufacturing costs, due to the completion of the Company's clinical trials in 2025.

 

General and administrative expenses were $4.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $1.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase of $3.0 million in general and administrative expenses during the three months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily due to a $2.2 million increase in stock-based compensation expense. There was also an increase of $0.4 million in professional fees and an increase in insurance costs of $0.4 million.

 

During the three months ended September 30, 2026, Cypherpunk recorded an unrealized gain of $330.8 million on the change in fair value of embedded derivative, reflecting the increase in the price of ZEC from $400.09 per ZEC at June 30, 2026 to $1,414.56 per ZEC at September 30, 2026.

 

As of September 30, 2026, Cypherpunk had cash and cash equivalents of $56.0 million and ZEC treasury holdings categorized as a digital asset receivable valued at $470.1 million.

About Cypherpunk

 

Cypherpunk Technologies is a privacy technology company. The Company's mission is to advance technologies that guarantee privacy for humans on the internet. Cypherpunk pursues this mission through three primary strategies: mining Zcash through Cypherpunk Mining; accumulating Zcash through open market purchases; and investing in, acquiring, and building technologies that push the frontier of privacy forward. Additionally, through its subsidiary Leap Therapeutics, the Company is developing novel therapies for patients with cancer by continuing the development of sirexatamab and FL-501. For more information about the Company, visit our websites at http://www.cypherpunk.com and http://www.leaptx.com or view our public filings with the SEC that are available via EDGAR at http://www.sec.gov.
