Group 1 Automotive Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Current quarter diluted earnings per common share from continuing operations of $8.59 and current quarter adjusted diluted earnings per common share from continuing operations (a non-GAAP measure) of $9.36
•Recently completed the purchase of 10 high volume and high margin dealerships from Hennessy Automobile Companies, expanding our presence in the fast-growing Atlanta market to 15 dealerships
•Disposition plan supporting our strategy to reduce leverage on track: completed U.S. dispositions with approximately $235 million in proceeds since quarter end, with an additional $125 million in proceeds expected from dispositions under definitive agreement, totaling approximately $360 million of proceeds
•All time record customer pay revenues and repair order count in the U.S.
HOUSTON, TX, October 28, 2026 — Group 1 Automotive, Inc. (NYSE: GPI) (“Group 1” or the “Company”), a Fortune 250 automotive retailer with 256 dealerships located in the U.S. and U.K., today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026 (“current quarter”).
“We’re pleased to have closed the Hennessy acquisition and grateful to the Hennessy family for 62 years of excellent stewardship,” said Daryl Kenningham, Group 1's President and Chief Executive Officer. “This acquisition aligns with our strategy to focus on high-revenue, premium brand stores in growing markets where we have a strong local presence. We have been deliberately reshaping our portfolio into larger, more profitable stores and seeing good progress selling stores that don’t fit our strategy. Proceeds from these sales will support our commitment to reduce our leverage.
Our focus on operating execution was evident in the third quarter. Year-over-year growth rates improved across key same store operating metrics compared to the second quarter, despite challenging market conditions. Importantly, in the U.S. we grew same-store customer pay revenue and repair orders 3% and 4% year-over-year, respectively, and continued to benefit from the cost-cutting actions we took earlier this year.”
Reconciliations for financial results, non-GAAP metrics and diluted earnings per common share between continuing and discontinued operations are included in the accompanying financial tables.
Current Quarter Results Overview
•Current quarter total revenues were $5.4 billion, compared to $5.8 billion for the third quarter of 2025 (“prior-year quarter”).
•Current quarter net income from continuing operations was $102.9 million, compared to $13.1 million for the prior-year quarter.
•Current quarter adjusted net income from continuing operations (a non-GAAP measure) was $112.1 million, compared to $135.1 million for the prior-year quarter.
•Current quarter diluted earnings per common share from continuing operations was $8.59, compared to $1.02 for the prior-year quarter.
•Current quarter adjusted diluted earnings per common share from continuing operations (a non-GAAP measure) was $9.36, compared to $10.45 for the prior-year quarter.

U.S. Operations Update
•U.S. parts and service customer pay revenues for the current quarter were $305.5 million, a 0.6% increase, compared to $303.5 million for the prior-year quarter. On a same store basis, U.S. parts and service customer pay revenues for the current quarter were $297.4 million, a 3.2% increase, compared to $288.2 million for the prior-year quarter.
•U.S. SG&A expenses for the current quarter were $452.1 million, a 6.0% decrease, compared to $481.2 million for the prior-year quarter. U.S. adjusted SG&A expenses for the current quarter were $445.2 million, a 5.4% decrease, compared to $470.7 million for the prior-year quarter.
•U.S. used vehicle GP PRU for the current quarter was $1,527, a 0.2% decrease, compared to $1,529 for the prior-year quarter. On a same store basis, U.S. used vehicle GP PRU for the current quarter was $1,544, a 1.6% increase, compared to $1,520 for the prior-year quarter.
U.K. Operations Update
•U.K. new vehicle retail units sold for the current quarter were 15,984, a 1.9% increase, compared to 15,687 for the prior-year quarter. On a same store basis, U.K. new vehicle retail units sold for the current quarter were 15,467, an 8.1% increase, compared to 14,308 for the prior-year quarter.
•U.K. used vehicle retail GP PRU for the current quarter was $1,437, a 15.7% increase, compared to $1,242 for the prior-year quarter. On a same store basis, U.K. used vehicle retail GP PRU for the current quarter was $1,428, a 13.9% increase, compared to $1,254 for the prior-year quarter.
•U.K. parts and service revenues for the current quarter were $162.3 million, a 2.4% decrease, compared to $166.3 million for the prior-year quarter. On a same store basis, U.K. parts and service revenues for the current quarter were $159.7 million, a 6.4% increase, compared to $150.2 million for the prior-year quarter.

U.S. Corporate Development
•On July 30, 2026, the Company entered into a Purchase and Sale Agreement (the “Purchase Agreement”) to acquire substantially all of the assets, including real estate, of Hennessy Automobile Companies, Inc., headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia (the “Hennessy Acquisition”). On October 19th, 2026, the Company completed the Hennessy Acquisition, with total expected annual revenues of approximately $1.7 billion.
•Year to date, the Company has acquired 14 dealership operations, inclusive of the Hennessy Acquisition, in the U.S. with total expected annual revenues of approximately $2 billion. The Company remains focused on efficiently and effectively integrating acquisitions into existing operations to create value for shareholders.
•The Company also completed the disposition of two U.S. dealerships in July 2026 that were acquired during the second quarter with the intention of reselling. Year-to-date total annualized revenues associated with dealership dispositions for the Company in the U.S. were approximately $505 million.
•Subsequent to the current quarter, consistent with our disposition strategy, the Company completed the dispositions of a Lexus, Acura and Honda dealership in the U.S., which generated approximately $305 million in annualized revenues, for proceeds of approximately $235 million. Additionally, the Company has executed agreements to dispose of four dealerships in the U.S., which generated approximately $235 million of annualized revenues, for expected proceeds of approximately $125 million.
U.K. Corporate Development
•During the current quarter, the Company disposed of a Volkswagen and an Audi dealership in the U.K. These dealerships generated approximately $100 million in annualized revenues, bringing year-to-date total annualized revenues associated with dealership dispositions for the Company in the U.K. to approximately $495 million.
•The Company reached an agreement with Jaguar Land Rover (“JLR”) in the U.K. to revoke its previous election to terminate 10 JLR franchise agreements, which would have been effective as of August 2027. As a result, the Company will retain and operate the five remaining JLR dealerships in the U.K. it had previously elected to terminate.
•The Company expanded its U.K. network through an agreement with Geely, opening two of three planned locations to date, with the third expected by December 2026. Subsequent to the current quarter, the Company agreed with Chinese automaker Chery to open an Omoda & Jaecoo location in the U.K., also expected by December 2026.
•Year to date, the Company has acquired and integrated three dealership operations in the U.K. with total expected annual revenues of approximately $135 million.
Share Repurchases
The Company did not repurchase any shares of its common stock during the current quarter. During the current year, the Company repurchased 205,190 shares of common stock, representing approximately 1.7% of shares outstanding as of January 1, 2026, at an average price of $353.08 per share, for a total cost of $72.4 million, excluding excise taxes of $0.5 million.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had 11,947,272 shares of common stock and unvested restricted stock awards outstanding in the aggregate, and $306.3 million remaining under its Board authorized share repurchase program.
Future repurchases may be made from time to time, based on market conditions, legal requirements and other corporate considerations in the open market, pursuant to Rule 10b5-1 trading plans or in privately negotiated transactions, and subject to Board approval and covenant restrictions.

ABOUT GROUP 1 AUTOMOTIVE, INC.
Group 1 owns and operates 256 automotive dealerships, 319 franchises, and 33 collision centers in the United States and the United Kingdom that offer 38 brands of automobiles. NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES, SAME STORE DATA, AND OTHER DATA
In addition to evaluating the financial condition and results of our operations in accordance with U.S. GAAP, from time to time our management evaluates and analyzes results and any impact on the Company of strategic decisions and actions relating to, among other things, cost reduction, growth, profitability improvement initiatives, and other events outside of normal, or "core," business and operations, by considering alternative financial measures not prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. In our evaluation of results from time to time, we exclude items that do not arise directly from core operations, such as non-cash asset impairment charges, accelerated depreciation, out-of-period adjustments, legal matters, severance and restructuring charges, acquisition costs, non-cash gains and losses on interest rate swaps, gains and losses on dealership franchise or real estate transactions, and catastrophic events, such as hailstorms, hurricanes and snow storms. Because these non-core charges and gains materially affect the Company's financial condition or results in the specific period in which they are recognized, management also evaluates, and makes resource allocation and performance evaluation decisions based on, the related non-GAAP measures excluding such items. This includes evaluating measures such as adjusted selling, general and administrative expenses, adjusted net income, adjusted diluted earnings per share, adjusted operating margin, adjusted pretax margin, adjusted finance and insurance gross profit per retail unit and constant currency. These adjusted measures are not measures of financial performance under U.S. GAAP, but are instead considered non-GAAP financial performance measures. Non-GAAP measures do not have definitions under U.S. GAAP and may be defined differently by, and not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by, other companies. As a result, any non-GAAP financial measures considered and evaluated by management are reviewed in conjunction with a review of the most directly comparable measures calculated in accordance with U.S. GAAP. We caution investors not to place undue reliance on such non-GAAP measures, but also to consider them with the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measures.
In addition to using such non-GAAP measures to evaluate results in a specific period, management believes that such measures may provide more complete and consistent comparisons of operational performance on a period-over-period historical basis and a better indication of expected future trends. Our management also uses these adjusted measures in conjunction with U.S. GAAP financial measures to assess our business, including communication with our Board of Directors, investors, and industry analysts concerning financial performance. We disclose these non-GAAP measures, and the related reconciliations, because we believe investors use these metrics in evaluating longer-term period-over-period performance, and to allow investors to better understand and evaluate the information used by management to assess operating performance. The exclusion of certain expenses in the calculation of non-GAAP financial measures should not be construed as an inference that these costs are unusual or infrequent. We anticipate excluding these expenses in the future presentation of our non-GAAP financial measures.
In addition, we evaluate our results of operations on both an as reported and a constant currency basis. The constant currency presentation, which is a non-GAAP measure, excludes the impact of fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates. We believe providing constant currency information provides valuable supplemental information regarding our underlying business and results of operations, consistent with how we evaluate our performance. We calculate constant currency percentages by converting our current period reported results for entities reporting in currencies other than U.S. dollars using comparative period exchange rates rather than the actual exchange rates in effect during the respective periods. The constant currency performance measures should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, the measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. The Same Store amounts presented include the results of dealerships for the identical months in each period presented in comparison, commencing with the first full month in which the dealership was owned by us and, in the case of dispositions, ending with the last full month it was owned by us. Same Store results also include the activities of our corporate headquarters.
