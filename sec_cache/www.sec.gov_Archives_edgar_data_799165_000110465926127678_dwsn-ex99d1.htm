DAWSON GEOPHYSICAL REPORTS

THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

​

MIDLAND, Texas, November 16, 2026/PR Newswire/Dawson Geophysical Company (NASDAQ: DWSN) (the “Company”) today reported unaudited financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

​

Third Quarter 2026 Results

​

●Recognized fee revenue of $10.8 million, a 27% decrease over the third quarter of 2025
●Net loss of $5.2 million, $0.17 per share, which included $0.8 million of strategic transaction costs
●Negative Adjusted EBITDA of $2 million, compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $0.2 million in the third quarter of 2025

​

Year-to-Date 2026 Results

​

●Increased fee revenue 47% to $57.4 million
●Net loss of $0.9 million, $0.03 per share, which included $3.2 million of strategic transaction costs
●Generated Adjusted EBITDA of $9.5 million, a 580% increase over the nine months ended September 30, 2025

​

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below for our definition and reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA.

Management Comment

​

Tony Clark, Dawson’s President and CEO, commented, “Although our financial performance fell short of our expectations in the third quarter, I cannot be more excited about how this Company is positioned to capitalize on the renewed demand for seismic data acquisition services in North America. We are observing increased demand in Canada and in the United States across the oil and gas space and in other non-traditional markets, such as Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (“CCUS”) and geothermal, and we are scheduled to start our first geothermal data acquisition in November. In the oil and gas industry, we are seeing an increase in demand from major oil and gas companies, specifically for high-density channel count jobs. If this trend continues, we are uniquely positioned to capitalize on this based on our inventory of channels and vibrator energy source units. This renewed demand has strengthened our backlog of contracted jobs and will be evident in our future financial results. I evaluate the Company’s performance over the long term rather than quarterly, and 2026 will be the third year in a row of increasing top-line revenue, improved gross margins and higher Adjusted EBITDA, and I expect that to continue in 2027.”

​

Third Quarter and Year-to-Date Results

​

For the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported revenues of $11.4 million, a decrease of 50% compared to $22.7 million for the comparable quarter ended September 30, 2025. Revenue included reimbursable revenue of $0.6 million and $7.8 million for the quarters ended September 30, 2026, and September 30, 2025, respectively. Gross margin1 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was 2% compared to 15% for the comparable quarter ended September 30, 2025.

We incurred a net loss of $5.2 million or $0.17 per common share and generated negative Adjusted EBITDA of $2 million in the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $0.2 million in the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, we generated a net loss of $0.9 million or $0.03 per common share compared to a net loss of $2.5 million or $0.08 per common share for the same period of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, we generated Adjusted EBITDA of $9.5 million, compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $1.4 million in the same period of 2025.

​

Operations Update

​

The Company had two large channel crew jobs scheduled to start in the third quarter, which were delayed until the fourth quarter due to government permitting issues. We had two small channel crews operating during the third quarter.

We are scheduled to have three large channel crews deployed in November, with three additional small channel crews in the United States. We resumed our Canadian operations in October. The trend that we have observed in the United States for high-density channel count jobs is now evident in the Canadian market. We are scheduled to perform our first geothermal data acquisition in November.

We continue to schedule and bid larger channel count jobs due to our inventory of single-node channels. Additionally, we have seen an increase in activity related to non-traditional seismic exploration including geothermal CCUS seismic monitoring, and other rare minerals.

Capital Budget and Liquidity

​

The Company's Board of Directors approved a capital budget of $3 million for 2026, including the final payment under the single node purchase of $0.9 million, which was made in January 2026.

As of September 30, 2026, our cash position was $2.8 million, our working capital deficit was $6.2 million, compared to a deficit of $5 million at December 31, 2025, and our credit facility was fully drawn with an outstanding balance of $3.6 million. We believe that our cash on hand and operating cash flows are sufficient to fund our cash flow requirements as well as our debt obligations. As of October 31, 2026, our cash position was $6.0 million, and our credit facility was fully drawn with an outstanding balance of $3.5 million.

About Dawson

 

Dawson Geophysical Company is a leading provider of North American onshore seismic data acquisition services with operations throughout the continental United States and Canada. Dawson acquires and processes 2-D, 3-D and multi-component seismic data solely for its clients, ranging from major oil and gas companies to independent oil and gas operators, critical mineral and other non-traditional operators as well as providers of multi-client data libraries.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

In an effort to provide investors with additional information regarding the Company’s preliminary and unaudited results as determined by U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the Company has included in this press release information about the Company’s Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure as defined by Regulation G promulgated by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as its net income, before (i) interest expense, net, (ii) income tax expense or benefit, (iii) depreciation and amortization and (iv) non-recurring and other charges that it believes are not indicative of the Company’s core operating performance, such as strategic transaction costs. The Company uses Adjusted EBITDA as a supplemental financial measure to assess:

 

·

the financial performance of its assets without regard to financing methods, capital structures, taxes or historical cost basis;

​

​

 

·

its liquidity and operating performance over time in relation to other companies that own similar assets and that the Company believes calculate Adjusted EBITDA in a similar manner; and

​

 

 

·

the ability of the Company’s assets to generate cash sufficient for the Company to pay potential interest costs.

​

 The Company also understands that such data are used by investors to assess the Company’s performance. However, the term Adjusted EBITDA is not defined under U.S. GAAP, and Adjusted EBITDA is not a measure of operating income or operating performance presented in accordance with GAAP. When assessing the Company’s operating performance, investors and others should not consider this data in isolation or as a substitute for net income, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, cash flow from operating activities or other cash flow data calculated in accordance with GAAP. In addition, the Company may modify how it calculates Adjusted EBITDA, and its use of Adjusted EBITDA may not be comparable to Adjusted EBITDA or similarly titled measures utilized by other companies since other companies may not calculate Adjusted EBITDA in the same manner as the Company. Further, the results presented by Adjusted EBITDA cannot be achieved without incurring the costs that the measure excludes: interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, and other charges such as strategic transaction costs. A reconciliation of the Company’s Adjusted EBITDA to its net loss is presented in the table following the text of this press release.

​

As of September 30, 2026, Wilks Brothers, LLC (“Wilks”) and its affiliates control approximately 80% of our common stock. There is no guarantee that we will enter into a definitive agreement with any such parties regarding any such transaction. The terms of any potential agreement between us and Wilks, and/or any of its affiliates, would be contingent on certain conditions, including completion of due diligence and the negotiation of definitive transaction documents. Our Board of Directors has formed a special committee of independent directors to evaluate, negotiate and make recommendations to the Board regarding any such transaction with Wilks and/or its affiliates, including whether to pursue or decline to pursue any proposed transaction.
