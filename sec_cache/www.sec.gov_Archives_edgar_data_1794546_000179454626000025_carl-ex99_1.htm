Carlsmed® Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Raises Full-Year Guidance

 

Third quarter 2026 revenue of $21.6 million, representing 65% growth year-over-year

 

Full year 2026 revenue guidance raised to $78 million to $80 million

 

CARLSBAD, CALIFORNIA, NOVEMBER 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Carlsmed, Inc. (Nasdaq: CARL) (“Carlsmed” or the “Company”), a medical technology company pioneering AI-enabled personalized spine surgery solutions, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“The third quarter represented strong execution against our long-term strategy as we delivered 65% revenue growth and advanced our leadership position in AI-enabled personalized surgery as we grew our surgeon user base by more than 60% over the prior year,” said Mike Cordonnier, Chairman and CEO of Carlsmed. "We broadened our personalized surgery platform, strengthened our leadership team, and secured new inpatient CMS reimbursement for our aprevo® lumbar spine fusion procedure. Taken together with our differentiated, capital-light model, these accomplishments reinforce our foundation for sustainable, profitable long-term growth as we head into 2027."

 

Recent Business Highlights

 

•
CMS Inpatient Prospective Payment System (IPPS) rule for fiscal year 2027 became effective October 1, 2026, with increased reimbursement for inpatient aprevo® lumbar procedures.
•
Launched aprevo® Lumbar Bi-lateral Posterior System in October, which the Company estimates addresses approximately 30,000 U.S. lumbar fusion procedures annually.
•
Further strengthened leadership team to drive productivity, scale, and patient-centric innovation with the promotion of Jeff Bertolini to Chief Operating Officer, the appointment of Rich Heppenstall as Chief Financial Officer, and the addition of Tony Jarc, Ph.D., as Chief Product and AI Officer.
•
Raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to a range of $78 million to $80 million, over 56% growth at the midpoint versus full-year 2025.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

•
Revenue was $21.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, a 65% increase compared to $13.1 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

•
Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was $16.7 million compared to $9.9 million for the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin was 77.3% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 75.9% in the third quarter of 2025.

 

•
Operating expenses were $29.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $19.0 million for the third quarter of 2025, and included $2.8 million in expenses related to executive transition costs and the expected resolution of certain legal matters. Operating expenses consisted of:

 

 

 

•
Research and development expenses of $6.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $4.4 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

•
Sales and marketing expenses of $13.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $9.6 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

•
General and administrative expenses of $10.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $4.9 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

•
Net loss was ($12.2) million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to ($8.5) million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

•
Adjusted EBITDA was ($7.3) million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to ($8.2) million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

•
Cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, short-term investments, and marketable securities were $80.3 million as of September 30, 2026.

 

2026 Financial Outlook

 

Revenue for the full year 2026 is expected to be in the range of $78 million to $80 million, representing growth of over 56% at the midpoint. This compares to prior annual revenue guidance of $74 to $78 million.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This press release contains certain financial information that is not presented in conformity with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), including EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net loss and adjusted net loss per share. The non-GAAP financial measures are provided as supplemental information to Carlsmed’s financial measures presented in this press release that are calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP.

 

The Company calculates EBITDA as net income (loss), adjusted to exclude: (i) net interest (income) expense, (ii) income tax expense (benefit), (iii) depreciation expense from property and equipment, and (iv) amortization expense from long-lived assets. We define “Adjusted EBITDA” as EBITDA adjusted to exclude (i) stock-based compensation expense, (ii) acquisition and transaction costs, (iii) acquired intangible asset amortization and impairments, (iv) restructuring and cost reduction initiatives, (v) executive transition costs, (vi) legal settlements net of proceeds from insurance in excess of legal fees, (vii) changes in fair value of warrant liabilities and gains and losses on debt extinguishments or modifications, and (viii) non-operating investment gains and losses.

 

 

 

We define “Adjusted Net Loss” as net loss, adjusted to exclude: (i) acquisition and transaction costs, (ii) acquired intangible asset amortization and impairments, (iii) restructuring and cost reduction initiatives, (iv) executive transition costs, (v) legal settlements net of proceeds from insurance in excess of legal fees, (vi) change in fair value of warrant liabilities and gains and losses on debt extinguishments or modifications, and (vii) non-operating investment gains and losses. We define “Adjusted Net Loss Per Share” as Adjusted Net Loss divided by the weighted-average number of shares of common stock outstanding during the period, adjusted to exclude shares that were accelerated upon executive severance. Each item excluded in a period is separately identified and quantified in the reconciliation that follows.

 

These non-GAAP measures are presented because management believes they allow investors to view the Company’s performance in a manner similar to the method used by management to evaluate financial performance for both strategic and annual operating planning. Management believes that to properly understand short-term and long-term financial trends, it is helpful for investors to understand the impact of the items excluded from the calculation of non-GAAP metrics, in addition to considering the Company’s GAAP financial measures. Management believes that the excluded items are not reflective of the Company’s ongoing core business operations and financial condition. Excluding such items allows investors and analysts to compare our operating performance to other companies in our industry and to compare the Company’s period-over-period results.

 

The non-GAAP financial measures used by Carlsmed may not be the same or calculated in the same manner as those used and calculated by other companies. Non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for Carlsmed’s financial results prepared and reported in accordance with GAAP. These non-GAAP measures should not be construed as an inference that the Company’s future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. We urge investors to review the reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measures to the comparable GAAP financial measures included in this press release, and not to rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business. Reconciliations of EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA, as well as Adjusted Net Loss and Adjusted Net Loss Per Share, are reported in this press release to the most comparable GAAP measure for the respective periods and appears in the table captioned “Reconciliation of GAAP Net Loss to Adjusted EBITDA” and “Reconciliation of GAAP Net Loss to Adjusted Net Loss Per Share” respectively. Within the accompanying financial tables presented, certain columns and rows may not add due to the use of rounded numbers.
