Red River Bancshares, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
ALEXANDRIA, Louisiana, October 30, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Red River Bancshares, Inc. (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: RRBI), the holding company for Red River Bank (the “Bank”), announced today its unaudited financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $12.6 million, or $1.90 per diluted common share (“EPS”), an increase of $800,000 or 6.8%, compared to $11.8 million, or $1.78 EPS, for the second quarter of 2026, and an increase of $1.8 million, or 16.3%, compared to $10.8 million, or $1.63 EPS, for the third quarter of 2025. For the third quarter of 2026, the quarterly return on assets was 1.48%, and the quarterly return on equity was 12.84%.
Net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $36.3 million, or $5.49 EPS, an increase of $4.9 million, or 15.8%, compared to $31.3 million, or $4.65 EPS, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the return on assets was 1.45%, and the return on equity was 12.73%.
Third Quarter 2026 Performance and Operational Highlights
The third quarter of 2026 financial results included record-high quarterly net income and EPS along with increased loans, deposits, and assets. We also completed our New Orleans market banking center relocation project and continued to execute our plan to expand organically.
•Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $12.6 million, up $800,000, or 6.8%, from the prior quarter. Net income for the third quarter benefited from a $916,000 increase in net interest income.
•Net interest income increased $916,000, or 3.2%, mainly as a result of higher earning asset balances and one more accrual day in the quarter. Net interest margin fully taxable equivalent (“FTE”) was consistent at 3.61% for the third quarter of 2026 and for the prior quarter.
•As of September 30, 2026, assets were $3.37 billion, up $56.2 million, or 1.7%, from $3.31 billion as of June 30, 2026, as a result of a $56.5 million increase in deposits.
•Deposits totaled $2.96 billion as of September 30, 2026, up $56.5 million, or 1.9%, from $2.91 billion as of June 30, 2026. This increase was primarily due to higher commercial deposit account balances.
•As of September 30, 2026, loans held for investment (“HFI”) were $2.32 billion, up $59.5 million, or 2.6%, from $2.26 billion as of June 30, 2026. In the third quarter of 2026, new loan originations and construction commitment fundings exceeded payments and payoffs.
•In the third quarter of 2026, nonperforming assets (“NPA(s)”) increased $3.0 million, or 113.4%, to $5.6 million, or 0.17% of assets, as of September 30, 2026, primarily due to a commercial relationship that we are actively working to resolve. The provision for loan losses was lower in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the previous quarter.
•We paid a quarterly cash dividend of $0.25 per common share in the third quarter of 2026.
•The 2026 stock repurchase program authorizes us to purchase up to $10.0 million of our outstanding shares of common stock from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026. There was no stock repurchase activity between January 1, 2026 and September 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, the 2026 stock repurchase program had $10.0 million of available capacity.
•We continued to implement our organic expansion plan with the following projects:
◦In the New Orleans market, we completed our banking center relocation project. We remodeled a portion of the ground floor of the Energy Centre Building on Poydras Street. On July 20, 2026, we relocated the Baronne Street retail banking center and the New Orleans market leadership and lenders to this updated, convenient, and visible location.
◦In the Acadiana market, construction is in process on our second full-service banking center in this market, located on Camellia Boulevard in Lafayette, Louisiana. We expect this location to open in the first quarter of 2027.
◦On October 22, 2026, we purchased a former credit union building located on Gause Boulevard E. in Slidell, Louisiana. We expect to open this location in the first quarter of 2027 as the Bank’s first full-service banking center location in Slidell and our second in the Northshore market.
•In the third quarter of 2026, S&P Global Market Intelligence ranked the Bank within the top 50 best deposit franchises as of June 30, 2026, for banks with assets between $3.0 and $10.0 billion.

Net Interest Income and Net Interest Margin FTE
Net interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $29.9 million, which was $916,000, or 3.2%, higher than the second quarter of 2026 and benefited from one more accrual day. Net interest income increased due to higher average balances and yields on earning assets, partially offset by a higher cost of deposits and average deposit balances. Interest income on loans increased $1.0 million due to higher average loan balances and yields. For the third quarter of 2026, the average rate on new and renewed loans was 6.47%. Interest income on short-term liquid assets increased $567,000, primarily due to higher average balances. Interest expense increased by $558,000 due to adjusting selected deposit rates in response to rate competition, as well as higher interest-bearing transaction deposit balances.
Net interest margin FTE was 3.61% for the third quarter of 2026, which was consistent with the prior quarter. Net interest margin FTE improved slightly at the end of the third quarter as a result of the higher interest rate environment.
The federal funds rate range was 3.50%-3.75% between December 2025 and September 2026. In September 2026, the Federal Open Market Committee increased the federal funds rate by 25 basis points (“bp(s)”), resulting in a range of 3.75%-4.00%. The market’s expectation is that the federal funds range may increase by another 25 bps in the fourth quarter of 2026. During the fourth quarter of 2026, we project $93.8 million of fixed rate loans at 5.85% to mature, which we expect to redeploy into loans with higher rates. We have $529.5 million of floating rate loans at 6.24%, which we expect to reprice slightly higher. We also expect to receive $24.0 million in securities cash flows at 3.64%, for which we are evaluating reinvestment options as we consider balance sheet management strategies and the forecasted interest rate environment. We project $190.7 million in time deposits at 3.37% to mature, which we expect to renew at higher rates. Selected transaction deposit rates could also be further adjusted in response to rate competition. Depending on balance sheet activity, the interest rate environment, and competition, we expect net interest income and net interest margin FTE to increase slightly in the fourth quarter of 2026.
Noninterest Income
Noninterest income totaled $4.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, up $186,000, or 3.9%, from the previous quarter.
The Small Business Investment Company (“SBIC”) partnerships reported a gain of $112,000 in the third quarter of 2026, compared to a loss of $291,000 in the previous quarter. These results were mainly due to fund value adjustments as an SBIC fund continues its wind-down phase. We expect SBIC income or loss to fluctuate in future quarters.
Mortgage loan income was $698,000 for the third quarter of 2026, down $230,000, or 24.8%, from the previous quarter, due to rising mortgage interest rates reducing mortgage activity.
Debit card income, net, was $1.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, consistent with the previous quarter. The third quarter of 2026 benefited from the receipt of a $50,000 periodic refund from our debit card provider, compared to a $63,000 similar refund in the previous quarter.

Operating Expenses
Operating expenses totaled $18.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, up $408,000, or 2.2%, from the previous quarter.
Personnel expenses totaled $10.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, up $206,000, or 1.9%, from the previous quarter. This increase was primarily due to an increase in headcount and higher personnel-related accruals. As of September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, we had 390 and 381 total employees, respectively.
Loan and deposit expenses totaled $426,000 for the third quarter of 2026, up $184,000, or 76.0%, from the previous quarter. The second quarter of 2026 benefited from reimbursement of $82,000 of collection expenses due to the successful resolution of nonaccrual loans.
Occupancy and equipment expenses totaled $1.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, consistent with the previous quarter. The third quarter of 2026 included $46,000 of nonrecurring expenses related to the relocation of banking centers and offices in the Northwest and New Orleans markets.
Legal and professional expenses totaled $487,000 for the third quarter of 2026, down $117,000, or 19.4% from the previous quarter. This decrease was due to lower audit and consulting expenses.
Loans
Loans HFI were $2.32 billion as of September 30, 2026 and $2.26 billion as of June 30, 2026. In the third quarter of 2026, new loan originations and construction commitment fundings exceeded payments and payoffs. As of September 30, 2026, we had $93.8 million of unfunded construction loan commitments, which we expect to fund over time.

Asset Quality and Allowance for Credit Losses
NPAs totaled $5.6 million as of September 30, 2026, an increase of $3.0 million, or 113.4%, from June 30, 2026, primarily due to a commercial relationship that we are actively working to resolve. The ratio of NPAs to assets was 0.17% and 0.08% as of September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, respectively.
The provision for credit losses for the third quarter of 2026 was $500,000, which was lower than the provision for credit losses of $750,000 for the prior quarter. The decrease was driven by stable charge-off trends and steady local economic conditions affecting the allowance for credit losses (“ACL”) calculation, partially offset by the increase in NPAs. As of September 30, 2026, the ACL was $25.1 million. The ratio of ACL to loans HFI was 1.08% as of September 30, 2026 and 1.09% as of June 30, 2026. The net charge-offs to average loans ratio was 0.01% for the third quarter of 2026 and 0.00% for the prior quarter.

About Red River Bancshares, Inc.
Red River Bancshares, Inc. is the bank holding company for Red River Bank, a Louisiana state-chartered bank established in 1999 that provides a fully integrated suite of banking products and services tailored to the needs of our commercial and retail customers. Red River Bank operates from a network of 28 banking centers throughout Louisiana and two combined loan and deposit production offices, one each in Lafayette, Louisiana, and Shreveport, Louisiana. Banking centers are located in the following Louisiana markets: Central, which includes the Alexandria metropolitan statistical area (“MSA”); Northwest, which includes the Shreveport-Bossier City MSA; Capital, which includes the Baton Rouge MSA; Southwest, which includes the Lake Charles MSA; the Northshore, which includes the Slidell-Mandeville-Covington MSA; Acadiana, which includes the Lafayette MSA; and New Orleans, which includes the New Orleans-Metairie MSA.
