UNLOCK THE SKIES Q3 2026 SHAREHOLDER LETTER | 3 Dear Shareholders, Last quarter, I told you that we would soon begin showcasing our Midnight air taxi in major U.S. cities. Since then, our team executed the first part of Midnight’s “No Roads” tour, across seven cities in Northern California, including San Francisco and Oakland. Over several weeks, Midnight gave Northern California the longest look yet at what electric aviation can mean for life in our cities. The tour opened with a round trip between Salinas and Hollister. From there, Midnight worked its way through seven Northern California cities, hundreds of miles, and dozens of flights in all. The highlight of this part of the tour was Midnight’s multiple flights around San Francisco Bay via Oakland International Airport navigating complex airspace shared with three international airports, with the Golden Gate Bridge as the backdrop. One of my favorite shots from the tour so far is Midnight passing over Apple headquarters, where so much of the technology that changed daily life was born. Flying day after day, in some of the busiest airspace in the country, is how trust with the FAA gets built, and our team’s work resulted in Midnight receiving its Market Survey Special Airworthiness Certificate from the FAA. In 2026, Midnight progressed from piloted test flights over open country and lightly used airspace to piloted flights over densely populated cities in some of the busiest airspace in America. Our Market Survey Certificate allows us to mature to passenger trials and revenue operations under the eIPP and as a part of LA28, and each of those steps is a building block toward Type Certification and routine urban air mobility operations at scale. None of this happened by accident. The work to ready us for Midnight operations in Los Angeles has been underway for more than two years, since we announced our planned Los Angeles route network in August 2024. Eighteen months ago, LA28 selected Archer as the Official Air Taxi Provider of the 2028 Olympic Games, and a few weeks later, after working closely with industry leaders on the design of the program, the White House established the eVTOL Integration Pilot Program and a pathway for accelerated launch in American cities. Together, they set the strategy for bringing Midnight into service: fly in American cities through 2027, and serve the Games in 2028. That is why we chose Los Angeles as our launch city. At this time last year, we announced the acquisition of the master lease and ultimate control of Hawthorne Airport, minutes from LAX and the major LA sports and entertainment venues, and we have now operated the airport and its fixed-base operator for almost a year. That has given us unmatched real-world operational experience and a platform for working through air taxi airspace compatibility with the FAA in LA and beyond. We are rapidly modernizing the airport: in under a year we have upgraded the terminal building, brought electric aircraft charging to the airport, and begun constructing eleven new hangars.

 
UNLOCK THE SKIES Q3 2026 SHAREHOLDER LETTER | 4 This morning, we announced that we’ve delivered the first Midnight to Los Angeles for the next part of our "No Roads" tour, which we expect to begin in the coming weeks. It will fly out of a hangar at Hawthorne that once served as the world headquarters, and the original office and hangar, of Jack Northrop's Northrop Corporation, a company that helped write the last century of American aviation, and I can think of no better place from which to launch the next one. The timing couldn’t be better, with Los Angeles hosting the Super Bowl in February and the LA28 Games opening in a little over 600 days. With all of that as our foundation, we are confident that an air taxi commercialization strategy built around Los Angeles is the right one. Our commercial and infrastructure teams are assembling the Los Angeles network piece by piece, with planned vertiports connecting SoFi Stadium, USC, and FBOs across the region, and, as we announced in August, a downtown vertiport at L.A. LIVE with AEG. Los Angeles is where we are writing the blueprint for every city that follows. We continue to plan with the FAA to bring the “No Roads” tour to Texas, Florida, and New York, in close partnership with the Texas Department of Transportation, the Florida Department of Transportation, and the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey. As we have done in LA, New York, Miami and the Bay Area, we believe it is important to announce route networks well ahead of operations, so that our regulatory and infrastructure partners in each city have time to get ready alongside us. That is why, last month, we announced our planned Texas air taxi networks across Dallas, Houston, and Austin, alongside numerous infrastructure partners. On certification, Midnight is in the fourth and final phase of the FAA’s type certification process, the implementation phase, where every compliance finding is made against the means of compliance and test plans we agreed with the agency. It is the most rigorous stretch of the program, and the FAA team is working alongside us on compliance approvals spanning structures, aircraft-level systems, and the other key design features of the aircraft, with systems and equipment testing underway. We continue to make meaningful progress quarter over quarter. On piloted transition, we are in the final push: the aircraft is flying faster with each flight, and we are closing in on completing that milestone by the end of the year. Now let’s shift to talk about our pending acquisitions of Wisk, SkyGrid, and Insitu from Boeing. We have been working hard to get all of the government approvals necessary to close the acquisition. We previously indicated we expect to close it by the end of the year. However, we now believe that the closing will be much sooner than that, with only one of the nearly 20 governmental and third-party approvals outstanding. As I discussed last quarter, Wisk and SkyGrid are about accelerating the development, commercialization, and scaling of our air taxi business, while Insitu is about near-term revenue and profitability on the unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) side. Insitu is one of the most trusted names in intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance drones, a profitable business with over $200 million in annual revenue across 35 countries¹, 1 Based on Insitu’s current financials and financial estimates.

Our goal going into 2027 is to rapidly grow Insitu’s revenue base, which can then meaningfully offset our continued spend to bring Midnight to market. Integration planning has been underway for months, and the more time we spend with the Insitu team, the clearer its industry-leading position becomes. Expect to hear a lot more from us on the strategy once we are on the other side of closing. In parallel, we continue to advance our strategic partnership with Anduril. This quarter, we began testing the hybrid turboshaft propulsion system for that aircraft platform, and the program remains on track with first full-scale flight planned for late 2027. We’ve also now delivered the initial battery packs and electric engines to Anduril for Omen, the autonomous air vehicle they are building with the EDGE Group in the UAE. As I said last quarter, I am confident that deploying our technology early in defense will deliver value across the whole business, and we are beginning to see that play out. The excitement around our progress and products continues to bring in new partners around the world. ITA Airways, Italy's flag carrier and a member of the Lufthansa Group, became the first European airline to join our network, with plans to bring Midnight to Italy's major cities. We are announcing a new partnership this week: Archer is partnering with Formula 1 with the goal to explore the future integration of eVTOL air taxi services at Grand Prix events. We began building toward that experience in Austin last month, where Midnight was on display in the Fan Zone and the F1 Paddock, and we will be at the Las Vegas Grand Prix later this year. I started Archer to unlock the skies, and we are now doing that across both commercial and defense. With our first aircraft in Los Angeles and Boeing set to become our largest shareholder when our transaction closes, I believe we are building an end-to-end aerospace and defense platform that very few companies could assemble, and one that will matter for decades. I have never been more confident in the work we are doing at Archer. Adam Goldstein Founder & Chief Executive Officer
