AMNEAL REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
– Q3 2026 Net Revenue of $863 million; GAAP Net Loss of $22 million; Diluted Loss per Share of $(0.06) –
– Adjusted EBITDA of $207 million; Adjusted Diluted EPS of $0.26 –
– Updating 2026 Full Year Guidance –

BRIDGEWATER, NJ, November 3, 2026 - Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: AMRX) (“Amneal” or the “Company”) today announced its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Amneal delivered another quarter of strong, broad-based performance, enabling us to increase our 2026 guidance once more. As we prepare to enter 2027, our financial performance is accelerating driven by a number of key catalysts and new launches, including Women’s Health patches, biosimilars, injectables such as lanreotide, CREXONT®, and BREKIYA® autoinjector. The Kashiv integration is proceeding well, and we are excited about the long-term growth potential in biosimilars. With the breadth and depth of opportunities across our portfolio, we remain highly confident in Amneal’s robust growth outlook,” said Chirag and Chintu Patel, Co-Founders and Co-Chief Executive Officers of Amneal.

Third Quarter 2026 Results
Net revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $863 million, an increase of 10% compared to $785 million in the third quarter of 2025. Affordable Medicines net revenue increased 19%, driven by biosimilars, women’s health patches, and new launches. Specialty net revenue increased 20%, driven by key branded products, including CREXONT®, BREKIYA® autoinjector, and UNITHROID®. AvKARE net revenue decreased 17%, as expected, due to a non-recurring cardiovascular product launch in the prior year.

Net loss attributable to Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. was $22 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to net income of $2 million in the third quarter of 2025 as higher revenue and gross profit were more than offset by non-recurring acquisition and integration costs, and higher tax provision.

Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter of 2026 was $207 million, an increase of 30% compared to the third quarter of 2025, reflecting higher revenue, higher gross profit, and lower operating expenses.

Diluted loss per share in the third quarter of 2026 was $(0.06) compared to diluted income per share of $0.01 for the third quarter of 2025, due to the aforementioned factors and higher share count as a result of the Kashiv acquisition. Adjusted diluted earnings per share in the third quarter of 2026 was $0.26, an increase of 53% compared to $0.17 for the third quarter of 2025.

The Company presents GAAP and adjusted (non-GAAP) quarterly results. Please refer to the “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” section and the accompanying GAAP to non-GAAP reconciliation tables for more information.

About Amneal
Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: AMRX), headquartered in Bridgewater, New Jersey, is a diversified, global biopharmaceutical leader focused on expanding access to affordable and innovative medicines. Amneal was founded in 2002 by brothers and co-CEOs Chirag and Chintu Patel, and built on the belief that innovation only matters if it’s accessible. Today, Amneal has a diverse and growing portfolio of approximately 300 complex generic, specialty and biosimilar medicines, delivering more than 160 million prescriptions annually, primarily in the United States. Our Affordable Medicines segment spans retail generics, injectables, and biosimilars. Our Specialty segment provides branded treatments in neurology, including Parkinson’s disease and migraine, and endocrinology. Our AvKARE segment distributes pharmaceuticals and medical products to U.S. federal, retail, and institutional customers. For additional information, please visit amneal.com and follow us on LinkedIn.

Explanations for Non-GAAP Reconciliations
(1)Acquisition, site closure, and idle facility expenses for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 primarily included acquisition costs associated with the acquisition of Kashiv and rent for vacated properties. Acquisition, site closure, and idle facility expenses for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025 and year ended December 31, 2025 primarily included costs related to a planned facility closure and rent for vacated properties.
(2)Loss on refinancing for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily comprised of debt issuance costs and the write-off of unamortized debt issuance costs. In February 2026, the Company entered into a repricing amendment for the Company’s Term Loan Due 2032 (the “February 2026 Repricing Amendment”), which reduced the applicable interest rate margins on such term loan. In August 2026, the Company entered into another repricing amendment for the Company’s Term Loan Due 2032 (the “August 2026 Repricing Amendment”), which further reduced the applicable interest rate margins on such term loan. Also in August 2026, the Company borrowed an additional $350.0 million under Amendment No. 4 to the Company’s Term Loan Credit Agreement (the “Incremental Term Loans”) to fund a portion of the cash consideration for the Kashiv Acquisition. The loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 primarily reflects costs associated with the August 2026 Repricing Amendment and the Incremental Term Loans, while the loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 primarily reflects costs associated with the February 2026 Repricing Amendment, the August 2026 Repricing Amendment and the Incremental Term Loans. For 2025, loss on refinancing was primarily comprised of debt issuance costs associated with the portion of the Term Loan Due 2028 that was modified as part of the Company’s debt refinancing on August 1, 2025. Refer to Note 14. Debt in the Company’s 2025 Annual Report on Form 10-K for information about the Company’s debt as of December 31, 2025.
(3)For the three months ended September 30, 2026, charges related to legal matters, net were $7.5 million, primarily consisting of a charge related to government investigations of $8.0 million, partially offset by immaterial net credits related to several matters. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, charges related to legal matters, net were $16.3 million, primarily consisting of (i) a $21.2 million charge associated with certain states electing a 25% cash conversion in lieu of product under the Nationwide Opioids Settlement Agreement, partially offset by a $20.8 million discount recorded on the expected settlement payments as of the agreement’s effective date, (ii) net charges associated with antitrust class action litigation of $7.5 million, (iii) a charge related to government investigations of $8.0 million, and (iv) immaterial net charges related to several matters. For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, charges related to legal matters, including interest, net also included interest expense associated with the Nationwide Opioids Settlement Agreement.
