XTI Aerospace Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) (“XTI Aerospace,” “XTI,” or the “Company”), parent company of Drone Nerds, LLC, (“Drone Nerds”), a leading drone solutions platform serving commercial, enterprise and government customers, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and reiterated its outlook for 2026.

 

2026 Third Quarter Highlights (Inpixon results excluded and reflected in discontinued operations):

 

●Revenue of $35.3 million, up 8.8% sequentially from the second quarter 2026 and up 46.1% from third quarter 2025 proforma revenue, partially driven by September demand ahead of Section 232 tariffs implemented this year

 

●Gross profit of $7.7 million

 

●Gross profit as a percentage of revenue of approximately 21.7%, inclusive of one-time benefits, primarily relating to tariff recoveries. Excluding the nonrecurring amounts, the gross profit margin was 16.0%.

 

●G&A reduced to 29.2% of sales from 39.2% in the first six months of 2026. Cost realignment initiatives included a reduction in corporate and development-division headcount, consolidation of office footprint, and termination of non-core consulting, vendor and software contracts

 

●Adjusted EBITDA of $1.6 million compared to negative $4.9 million in the second quarter of 2026

 

“Our strategic pivot to focus on our Drone Nerds business is substantially complete,” said Jeremy Schneiderman, XTI Aerospace’s Interim Chief Executive Officer. “We have discontinued the operation of the XTI aircraft business, rightsized our corporate overhead and are focused on the significant opportunity in the commercial and enterprise drone market. We are uniquely positioned to help our customers manage their drone programs from aircraft payloads, software, fleet management and training, to service and repair.”

 

“We have continued to strengthen our product offerings over the last several months, adding pressure washing, airport detection and first responder programs to our portfolio. The growth opportunities we see across our portfolio combined with continued disciplined expense management is expected to lead to the transition to positive ongoing operating cash flows during the fourth quarter of 2026.”

 

2026 Financial Outlook and Guidance:

 

●Full year 2026 revenue of $125 million to $130 million

 

●Full year 2026 gross profit as a percentage of revenue of 17 percent to 19 percent

 

The Company’s outlook assumes, among other things, continued access to its asset-based credit facility and no material further changes in tariff policy, and is subject to the risks described below under “Cautionary Statement Regarding Forward-Looking Statements.”

 

 

 

2026 Third Quarter and Recent Events:

 

●Announced partnership with AirSight, a drone detection and airspace security software solutions provider, to expand Drone Nerds’ drone detection offerings within its enterprise solutions portfolio

 

●Introduced ABZ Innovation C30 High-Pressure Cleaning Drone to its Enterprise Portfolio

 

●Launched its Drone as First Responder (DFR) program, Evolver DFR, which gives police, fire, and emergency agencies everything they need to confidently send a drone to an emergency call

 

●Appointed Dr. Allan Evans as Chairman of the Board of Directors and Jeremy Schneiderman as Interim CEO

 

Liquidity and Reduction of Corporate Expenses

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $5.7 million of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents and $11.9 million of remaining availability on the borrowing base under its credit facility at Drone Nerds.

 

During the second and third quarters the Company took significant action to reduce its corporate and ADS headcount and related compensation, to consolidate its office footprint, and to terminate non-core consulting, vendor and software contracts. Average monthly operating cash outflows of the Company’s ADS and corporate operations (excluding Drone Nerds) during the third quarter were approximately $1.1 million per month, down approximately 42% from the second quarter, and 62% from the first quarter.   

 

The Company has also recently restructured $4.7 million of promissory notes, which had previously been in default and also secured a cross-default waiver from JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. on our revolving credit facility.

About XTI Aerospace, Inc.

 

XTI Aerospace, Inc. (“XTI”) (Nasdaq: XTIA) is focused on building a leading platform for commercial and government drone adoption in the growing unmanned aircraft systems (“UAS”) industry. Through its subsidiary, Drone Nerds, LLC, XTI supports enterprise and government customers in sectors including public safety, agriculture, construction, and energy across the drone program lifecycle, from hardware sourcing and deployment to training, service, repair, and the proprietary Always Flying™ program. Its established U.S. customer base and reputation as a trusted solutions provider position the company as a go-to-market partner for drone manufacturers and software developers seeking to scale in the domestic market.

 

For more information about XTI, please visit xtiaerospace.com and follow XTI on LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and YouTube.

EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA

 

XTI Aerospace defines EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest, income taxes, depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA adjusted for certain items including, (i) non-cash stock-based compensation expense; (ii) severance and restructuring charges; (iii) change in the fair value of warrant liability; and (iv) selected charges that are unusual or non-recurring.

 

The Company believes that EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA financial measures assist our board of directors, management, investors, and lenders in comparing our operating performance and establishing operational goals on a consistent basis across periods by removing the effects of our capital structure and other items that impact the comparability of financial results from period to period. We present EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA because we believe they provide useful information regarding the factors and trends affecting our business in addition to measures calculated under GAAP. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are non-GAAP financial measures and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information provided in accordance with GAAP. These non-GAAP financial measures may not be computed in the same manner as similarly titled measures used by other companies.
