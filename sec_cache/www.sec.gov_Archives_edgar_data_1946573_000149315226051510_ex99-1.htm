Nakamoto Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results

 

New Launches Across Media, Asset Management, and Events Broaden Operating Reach

 

Extends Kraken Facility Maturity; No Scheduled Principal Due in 2026

 

NASHVILLE, Tenn.—(BUSINESS WIRE)—Nakamoto Inc. (NASDAQ: NAKA) (“Nakamoto” or the “Company”), a Bitcoin company that owns and operates a portfolio of Bitcoin-native enterprises, today announced its financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

David Bailey, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Nakamoto, said, “We spent the third quarter building. We launched Bitcoin Magazine TV to add year-round sponsorship revenue, took UTXO into structured credit, and held Bitcoin Asia in Hong Kong. We also announced Intelligence 2027, our first event built around AI. i27 takes our events business behind The Bitcoin Conference into the intersection between AI, investment, enterprise adoption and policy.

 

“We are doing this work while Bitcoin trades well below its highs. Markets move in cycles, and we are building Nakamoto to perform through all of them, by extending our debt maturities and building operating businesses that earn revenue beyond our Bitcoin holdings. We are confident that these initiatives and our selective approach to deploying capital will help us drive sustainable earnings and long-term shareholder value.”

 

Q3 2026 Financial Highlights

 

  ● Total operating revenue of $(1.9) million, including $3.9 million from the Company’s operating businesses and $(5.8) million of net derivative revenue, as the rise in the price of Bitcoin reduced the value of the Company’s derivative positions.
  ● GAAP operating income of $91.9 million, primarily reflecting a $110.4 million gain on the change in fair value of digital assets.
  ● Net income of $85.5 million, or $4.07 per diluted share.
  ● Adjusted operating loss of $15.8 million, which includes net derivative revenue and excludes changes in the fair value of digital assets.
  ● Held 4,283 Bitcoin at September 30, 2026, with an aggregate fair value of $357.9 million.
  ● Subsequent to quarter end, on November 11, 2026, the Company extended the maturity of 60.0 million USDT of its 165.0 million USDT Kraken loan facility from December 4, 2026 to December 4, 2027. The remaining 105.0 million USDT continues to mature on June 30, 2027.

 

Q3 2026 Operational Highlights

 

BTC Inc.

 

  ● Launched Bitcoin Magazine TV (“BMTV”) on September 14, expanding the Company’s media business into daily live coverage of Bitcoin, global markets, macroeconomics, geopolitics and AI, and adding year-round sponsorship inventory.
  ● Held Bitcoin Asia 2026 in Hong Kong on August 27–28. The conference brought the Company’s events platform to an audience of investors, businesses, developers, and policymakers across the region.
  ● Extended institutional programming to Hong Kong through the announced Deal Day and Bitcoin for Corporations Symposium formats.
  ● Announced Intelligence 2027 (“i27”), our inaugural event catering to the intersection between AI development, investment, enterprise adoption and policy. The event is scheduled for April 22–23, 2027, at the Palace of Fine Arts in San Francisco.

 

 
 

 

UTXO Management GP, LLC (“UTXO”)

 

  ● Launched UTXO Preferred Income Strategies LP on July 1, 2026, following its formation announcement in April, expanding UTXO’s investment capabilities into structured credit. UTXO Preferred Income Strategies, LP invests primarily in preferred securities issued by publicly traded companies that hold Bitcoin. It has a Senior Class, which is allocated profits first up to a preferred return, and a Junior Class, which receives the remaining profits and bears losses first.
  ● Enhanced its research offerings with the publication of The Digital Credit Primer in July and The Bitcoin Treasury Company Primer in August, providing analysis of Bitcoin-linked preferred securities, corporate treasury strategies and capital structures.
  ● As of September 30, 2026, UTXO managed approximately $118 million in assets.

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

 

On September 30, 2026, Nakamoto held 4,283 Bitcoin with an aggregate fair value of approximately $357.9 million. Of these, 3,030 Bitcoin were pledged as collateral for the Company’s notes payable and 1,253 were unencumbered.

 

As of September 30, 2026, Nakamoto had a Net Leverage – Digital Assets Ratio of 41%, cash and cash equivalents of $19.4 million, and total debt of $164.9 million.


On November 11, 2026, the Company amended its 165.0 million USDT loan facility with Kraken to extend the maturity of 60.0 million USDT from December 4, 2026, to December 4, 2027. Following the amendment, 105.0 million USDT matures on June 30, 2027, and 60.0 million USDT matures on December 4, 2027, leaving no scheduled principal due in 2026. The amendment also allows the net value of the Company’s derivative positions held in the collateral account to count toward the facility’s margin requirements.

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s enterprise value was $319.0 million, calculated as market capitalization of $173.6 million based on common shares outstanding and the Company’s stock price of $9.46, plus notes payable of $164.9 million, less cash and cash equivalents of $19.4 million.

 

As of September 30, 2026, Nakamoto’s shares outstanding were 18,346,473 and fully diluted shares outstanding were 23,099,471.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This press release contains the following non-GAAP financial measures consisting of adjusted operating income (loss), net debt, Net Leverage – Digital Assets Ratio and fully diluted shares outstanding. We define adjusted operating income (loss) as operating income (loss) excluding the change in fair value of digital assets, impairment of goodwill, gains and losses on investments, depreciation and amortization, transaction-related compensation and transaction-related general and administrative expenses (“adjusted operating income (loss)”). Management uses adjusted operating income (loss) to assess the operating results of our businesses without the effects of changes in the fair value of our digital asset holdings, goodwill impairment, gains and losses on investments, depreciation and amortization and transaction-related items, and believes it is useful to investors for that purpose. Adjusted operating income (loss) excludes changes in the fair value of our digital asset holdings and gains and losses on investments but includes derivative revenue, which itself may be affected by movements in the price of Bitcoin. We define fully diluted shares outstanding as common shares outstanding and all options, warrants, holdback shares for the BTC Inc. and UTXO Management acquisitions, restricted stock units and shares to be issued upon delivery of letters of transmittal from BTC Inc. stockholders (“fully diluted shares outstanding”). This period-end measure differs from the weighted-average diluted share count of 21.029 million used to calculate GAAP earnings per share for the third quarter of 2026. We define net debt as notes payable, net, less cash and cash equivalents, and Net Leverage – Digital Assets Ratio as net debt divided by digital assets. Management believes these measures are useful to investors in evaluating the Company’s leverage relative to its digital asset holdings. Non-GAAP financial measures are financial measures that are derived from consolidated financial statements, but that are not presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”). Non-GAAP financial measures are subject to material limitations as they are not measurements prepared in accordance with GAAP, and are not a substitute for such measurements. We use these non-GAAP financial measures and other key metrics internally to facilitate analysis of our financial and business trends and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. We believe these non-GAAP financial measures, when taken collectively, may be helpful to investors because they provide consistency and comparability with past and future financial performance by excluding certain items that may not be indicative of our business, results of operations, or outlook. However, non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as an analytical tool and are presented for supplemental informational purposes only. They should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP. In particular, other companies, including companies in our industry, may report adjusted operating income (loss) and fully diluted shares outstanding, or similarly titled measures but calculate them differently, which reduces their usefulness as comparative measures.
