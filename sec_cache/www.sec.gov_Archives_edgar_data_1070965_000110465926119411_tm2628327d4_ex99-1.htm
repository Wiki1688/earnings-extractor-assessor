Georgia Banking Company Reports 2026 Third-Quarter Earnings

 

Third Quarter 2026 Results and the First Full Quarter of Combined Operations Following the Tandem Bancorp, Inc. Merger

 

ATLANTA, Ga., October 28, 2026 /ACCESS Newswire/ -- Georgia Banking Company, Inc. (“GBC” or the “Company”), the holding company for Georgia Banking Company (the “Bank”), today reported net income of $6.3 million, or $0.57 per diluted common share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with net income of $5.6 million, or $0.60 per diluted common share, for the second quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 results included $2.0 million of direct listing expenses and $493 thousand of merger and acquisition expenses, each before taxes.

 

“Our third-quarter results reflect the strength of our core banking franchise, the execution of our growth strategy and the investments we have made in our people and capabilities,” said Bartow Morgan, Jr., Chief Executive Officer of GBC. “We continue to see attractive opportunities across our markets while maintaining a disciplined approach to credit, liquidity and capital. As our shares prepare to begin trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market, our priorities remain clear: deliver exceptional service to our clients, generate sustainable and profitable growth and create long-term value for our shareholders.”

 

2026 Third Quarter Highlights:

 

·Sequential and year-over-year comparisons are impacted by the merger with Tandem Bancorp, Inc. (“Tandem”), which closed on June 1, 2026 and was fully integrated on September 21, 2026;
·Pre-tax, pre-provision earnings were $10.5 million, an increase of $1.8 million, or 20.4%, from the second quarter 2026. Pre-tax, pre-provision operating earnings, which exclude direct listing and merger and acquisition expenses, were $12.9 million, an increase of $750 thousand, or 6.2%, from the prior quarter;
·Net interest income increased $2.3 million, or 8.0%, from the prior quarter;
·Net interest margin was 4.17% compared with 4.31% in the prior quarter, with the decrease primarily reflecting higher interest expense on other borrowed funds associated with the Company’s subordinated debt refinancing during the quarter;
·Average loans increased $158.3 million, or 6.7%, from the prior quarter to $2.5 billion. Loan growth was primarily attributable to an increase in commercial real estate and single-family residential loans from the prior quarter;
·Average total interest-bearing deposits increased $268.5 million, or 15.7%, from the prior quarter to $2.0 billion, inclusive of the impact of the Tandem acquisition;
·Noninterest-bearing deposits represented 26.0% of period-end total deposits. GBC’s average cost of total deposits nonannualized, including noninterest-bearing deposits, increased 2 basis points from the prior quarter to 0.58%, and decreased 7 basis points from the year-ago quarter;
·Provision for credit losses was $2.0 million compared to $1.1 million in the prior quarter and $734 thousand in third quarter 2025. Net charge-offs were 0.16% of average loans outstanding (annualized) for the quarter, while the nonperforming loan ratio was 0.99% of total loans held for investment at quarter end compared to 0.83% in the prior quarter. The allowance for credit losses (“ACL”) was $30.1 million, or 1.36% of total loans held for investment, at quarter end, an increase of $1.1 million from the prior quarter end;
·The Company maintained strong regulatory capital ratios at September 30, 2026. The Company’s preliminary Common Equity Tier 1 (“CET1”) ratio was 10.59%, compared to 10.29% at June 30, 2026 and 8.53% at September 30, 2025; and
·Named a Top Workplace by Axios for the fourth consecutive year, an honor based entirely on employee feedback gathered through a third-party survey.

 

 

 

 

 

 

Results of Operations for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

 

Net Interest Income and Net Interest Margin

 

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, net interest income before provision for credit losses was $31.4 million and net interest margin was 4.17% compared to $29.1 million and 4.31%, respectively, for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The approximately $2.3 million increase, or 8.0%, in net interest income before provision for credit losses was primarily due to a $4.6 million increase in interest income on loans held for investment, including fees, a $1.2 million increase in interest income on deposits with banks and federal funds sold and a $1.2 million decrease in interest expense on FHLB advances. The increase was partially offset by a $2.7 million increase in interest expense on transaction and savings accounts and a $1.6 million decrease in interest income on loans held for sale. Net interest margin decreased 14 basis points compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026 primarily due to an increase in interest expense on other borrowed funds as a result of restructuring our subordinated debt during the three months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, net interest income before provision for credit losses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 increased by $4.0 million, or 14.8%. The increase was primarily due to a $5.7 million increase in interest income on loans held for investment, including fees, a $2.0 million decrease in interest expense on FHLB advances and a $1.0 million increase in interest-bearing deposits with banks. The increase was partially offset by a $2.5 million increase in interest expense on transaction and savings accounts and a $2.4 million decrease in interest income on loans held for sale. Net interest margin decreased 20 basis points compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025 primarily due to a decrease in loan yields.

 

Noninterest Income

 

Noninterest income was $2.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of $451 thousand, or 13.8%, from the three months ended June 30, 2026. The decrease was primarily due to a $342 thousand decrease in mortgage banking activity.

 

Compared with the three months ended September 30, 2025, noninterest income increased $1.0 million, or 57.1%. The increase primarily reflected a $672 thousand increase in gain on sale of loans, a $298 thousand increase in service charges and fees, and a $286 thousand increase in mortgage banking activity, partially offset by decreases in other noninterest income categories.

 

Noninterest Expense

 

Noninterest expense was $23.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $23.7 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026, an increase of $104 thousand, or 0.4%. Excluding direct listing and merger and acquisition expenses, the change included a $717 thousand increase in salaries and employee benefits, primarily related to employees retained in connection with the Tandem acquisition and hiring associated with the Company’s direct listing on Nasdaq.

 

Compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, noninterest expense increased $5.3 million, or 29.0%. Excluding direct listing and merger and acquisition expenses, the increase primarily reflected a $1.8 million increase in salaries and employee benefits, primarily related to employees retained in connection with the Tandem acquisition and hiring associated with the Company’s direct listing on Nasdaq, and an $859 thousand increase in communications, data processing and equipment expense.

 

 

 

 

 

 

Financial Condition

 

Total loans, net, were $2.56 billion at September 30, 2026, an increase of $1.1 million from June 30, 2026. The change reflected $128.8 million of growth in loans held for investment, largely offset by a $126.5 million decrease in loans held for sale and the effect of related netting items included in total loans, net.

 

Total deposits were $2.70 billion at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $136.2 million from June 30, 2026. The decrease reflected a $171.8 million decline in interest-bearing deposits, partially offset by a $35.6 million increase in noninterest-bearing deposits.

 

Credit Quality

 

Nonperforming assets totaled $22.1 million, or 0.70% of total assets, at September 30, 2026, compared to $17.3 million, or 0.53% of total assets, at June 30, 2026. The Company recorded net charge-offs of $893 thousand for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Annualized net charge-offs to average loans outstanding were 16 basis points for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to no net charge-offs for the three months ended June 30, 2026 and 17 basis points for the three months ended September 30, 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, annualized net charge-offs to average loans outstanding were 13 bps compared to 7 bps for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

The allowance for credit losses as a percentage of loans held for investment was 1.36%, 1.39% and 1.43% at September 30, 2026, June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively. The Company recorded a provision for credit losses of $2.0 million, $1.1 million and $734 thousand for the three months ended September 30, 2026, June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively. The recorded provision for credit losses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026, was primarily attributable to an increase in general reserves as a result of changes in economic factors, portfolio mix and specific reserves. The balance for unfunded commitments for the three months ended September 30, 2026 remained relatively stable compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026 and the Company recorded no provision for or benefit from credit losses on unfunded commitments during the third quarter of 2026.

About Georgia Banking Company, Inc.

 

Georgia Banking Company is a community-focused financial institution headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and a wholly owned subsidiary of GBC. Under the leadership of CEO Bartow Morgan, Jr., a fifth-generation Georgia banker, the company has built a reputation as a trusted partner for businesses, professionals, and families in the markets it serves. By combining innovative solutions with a relationship-first approach, Georgia Banking Company remains committed to delivering exceptional service and supporting its communities.

We calculate: (a) tangible common equity as total shareholders’ equity, less goodwill and core deposit intangibles, net of accumulated amortization; (b) tangible assets as total assets less goodwill and core deposit intangibles, net of accumulated amortization; and (c) tangible common equity to tangible assets as tangible common equity (as described in clause (a)) divided by tangible assets (as described in clause (b)). For tangible common equity to tangible assets, the most directly comparable financial measure calculated in accordance with GAAP is total shareholders’ equity to total assets.

 

We believe that this measure is important to many investors in the marketplace who are interested in the relative changes from period to period in common equity and total assets, in each case, exclusive of changes in core deposit intangibles. Goodwill and other intangible assets have the effect of increasing both total shareholders’ equity and assets while not increasing our tangible common equity or tangible assets.

Return on Average Tangible Common Equity. Return on average tangible common equity is a non-GAAP measure generally used by financial analysts and investment bankers to evaluate financial institutions. We calculate: (a) net income available for common shareholders adjusted for amortization of core deposit intangibles (which we refer to as “return”) as net income, plus amortization of core deposit intangibles, less tax benefit at the statutory rate; (b) average tangible common equity as total average shareholders’ equity less average goodwill and average core deposit intangibles, net of accumulated amortization; and (c) return (as described in clause (a)) divided by average tangible common equity (as described in clause (b)). For return on average tangible common equity, the most directly comparable financial measure calculated in accordance with GAAP is return on average equity.

 

We believe that this measure is important to many investors in the marketplace who are interested in the return on common equity, exclusive of the impact of core deposit intangibles. Goodwill and core deposit intangibles have the effect of increasing total shareholders’ equity while not increasing our tangible common equity. This measure is particularly relevant to acquisitive institutions that may have higher balances in goodwill and core deposit intangibles than non-acquisitive institutions.
