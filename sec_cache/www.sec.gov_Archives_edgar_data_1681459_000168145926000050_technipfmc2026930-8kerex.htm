Total Company revenue in the third quarter was $2,895.8 million. Net income attributable to TechnipFMC was $418.4 million, or $1.05 per diluted share. Adjusted net income was $418.8 million, or $1.05 per diluted share.
Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes pre-tax charges and credits, was $627.8 million; adjusted EBITDA margin was 21.7 percent (Exhibit 8).

Doug Pferdehirt, Chair and CEO of TechnipFMC, remarked, “Our quarterly results reflect strong operational performance throughout the organization. Total Company revenue in the period was $2.9 billion, with adjusted EBITDA of $636 million, when excluding foreign exchange impacts. We generated free cash flow of $470 million. These results reflect consistency in our execution and reinforce customer trust and confidence in our capabilities.”

“We recently achieved several operational milestones, all further strengthening our track record of client success in advancing offshore projects. We delivered the first Subsea 2.0® tree for Chevron’s Gorgon Stage 3 project, representing the first tree delivered under our 20-year Subsea 2.0® frame agreement. We also celebrated the manufacture and delivery of the 200th subsea tree for ExxonMobil in Guyana. These examples highlight the collective efforts of our global teams, delivering projects on schedule—and in many cases ahead of schedule—giving our customers greater confidence in sanctioning offshore projects.”
Pferdehirt continued, “Turning to inbound orders, we announced three Subsea awards for the third quarter. We secured multiple contracts to supply flexible pipe systems, the first for Azule Energy in Angola, and the second for Petrobras in support of multiple pre-salt fields in Brazil. We were also awarded an integrated contract from PETRONAS for the Limbayong project, one of three iEPCI® awards in the period. Subsea orders in the quarter totaled $2.1 billion.”

Pferdehirt added, “We continue to enhance our customer relationships through collaborative partnerships. Our recent Strategic Collaboration Agreement with PETRONAS establishes TechnipFMC as their deepwater development partner, creating potential opportunities for growth across multiple projects over the coming years. We also announced a Memorandum of Understanding last week with bp, under which TechnipFMC will be the preferred strategic supplier of 20K systems for their Paleogene program in the Gulf of America.”

“Both of these agreements exemplify our focus on client relationships and reinforce the value of customer engagement. These relationships become increasingly more valuable as clients adopt a portfolio approach to offshore development, optimizing opportunities across their entire asset base rather than simply advancing individual projects.”

Pferdehirt continued, “Looking ahead, we remain optimistic about the durability of the subsea market. We have high visibility, supported by a sustained increase in deepwater activity—with significantly improved project economics—and a shift in operator capital toward prolific, high-quality offshore reservoirs. This outlook is reflected in our Subsea Opportunities List, which has increased for the ninth consecutive quarter to $32 billion, when using the midpoint of project values.”

Pferdehirt concluded, “The robust pipeline of opportunities supports our expectation for further growth, and if we extend our view to include 2027, we expect Subsea orders over the next five quarters to be at least $14.5 billion. The momentum in order growth should also lift the quality of our inbound, with substantially more Subsea 2.0®, iEPCI®, and direct awards expected in the year ahead. We also believe this marks the beginning of a sustained wave of activity that will extend beyond the end of the decade.”

Subsea reported third-quarter revenue of $2,600 million, an increase of 4.5 percent from the second quarter. The sequential revenue improvement was driven by increased project activity, specifically iEPCI® projects in Indonesia and Brazil, as well as increased services activity. The revenue increase was partially offset by lower project activity in Norway.
Subsea reported an operating profit of $522.6 million, an increase of 7.4 percent when compared to the second quarter. Operating results improved sequentially due to strong execution and higher project and services activity. Operating profit margin increased 50 basis points to 20.1 percent.
Subsea reported adjusted EBITDA of $611.6 million, an increase of 6 percent when compared to the second quarter. The factors impacting operating profit also drove the sequential increase in adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDA margin increased 30 basis points to 23.5 percent.

Subsea inbound orders were $2,051.5 million for the quarter. Book-to-bill in the period was 0.8x. The following awards were included in the period:
•Azule Energy West Hub Tails Project (Angola)
Significant* contract to supply flexible flowlines and risers by Azule Energy for the West Hub Tails project, an extension of the Block 15/06 Agogo Integrated Development. TechnipFMC will design and manufacture flexible flowlines, risers, and associated equipment to accommodate all production from the West Hub area for consolidation into the Agogo Floating Production, Storage, and Offloading vessel.
*A “significant” contract is between $75 million and $250 million.

•PETRONAS Limbayong iEPCI® Project (Malaysia)
Significant* integrated Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Installation (iEPCI®) contract by PETRONAS Carigali Sdn. Bhd. for the Limbayong deepwater project offshore Malaysia. This greenfield development will benefit from both our Subsea 2.0® configure-to-order platform and iEPCI® execution of the entire subsea scope, reducing project cycle time on the fast-track development.
*A “significant” contract is between $75 million and $250 million.

•Petrobras Flexibles Contract (Brazil)
Significant* contract by Petrobras, following a competitive bidding process, to supply flexible pipe systems supporting developments offshore Brazil. TechnipFMC will design, engineer, and manufacture flexible flowlines for use across multiple pre-salt fields. The project will draw on local engineering, manufacturing, and supply chain capabilities, further strengthening Brazil's offshore energy industry.
*A “significant” contract is between $75 million and $250 million.

•bp MoU for Supply of 20K Subsea Production Systems (Gulf of America)
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with BP Exploration & Production Inc., a subsidiary of BP p.l.c., which sets out a framework for bp and TechnipFMC to cooperate in connection with the supply of 20,000 psi (20K) subsea tree systems for bp’s Paleogene projects in the Gulf of America. The MoU allows both parties to identify and work cooperatively on early opportunities and future technology development in support of greater schedule certainty and improved project outcomes.

About TechnipFMC

TechnipFMC is a leading technology provider to the traditional and new energy industries; delivering fully integrated projects, products, and services.

With our proprietary technologies and comprehensive solutions, we are transforming our clients’ project economics, helping them unlock new possibilities to develop energy resources while reducing carbon intensity and supporting their energy transition ambitions.

Organized in two business segments — Subsea and Surface Technologies — we will continue to advance the industry with our pioneering integrated ecosystems (such as iEPCI®, iFEED® and iComplete®), technology leadership, and digital innovation.

Each of our approximately 22,000 employees is driven by a commitment to our clients’ success, and a culture of strong execution, purposeful innovation, and challenging industry conventions.

TechnipFMC uses its website as a channel of distribution of material company information. To learn more about how we are driving change in the industry, go to www.TechnipFMC.com and follow us on X @TechnipFMC.
